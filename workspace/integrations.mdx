Integrations connect Lynka to external services. A button or saved browser state is not proof that the connection is ready. Confirm the connection status shown by Lynka.

## Google and Gmail

Google integration settings are available from the Settings integration area. Follow the connection flow and confirm the status shown by Lynka.

Use the connection status shown in Lynka rather than an optimistic browser state.

If the connection does not complete, retry it from Settings and contact support if the error continues. A successful browser redirect is not proof that Gmail is connected.

## Record email

Email composition is available from supported record actions. Provider based sending and durable email history are related but separate concerns. Connecting Google does not replace Lynka's own record links, outbox or communication history.

## When an integration looks unavailable

Check the workspace capability, your access, and the current connection state. For Gmail, reconnect through the integration settings rather than assuming a cached Connected label is authoritative.
