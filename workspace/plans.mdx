Lynka Free Forever has no signup expiry. Choose a plan based on the features, collaboration, and LeadGen capacity your business needs.

## Free Forever

Free includes one user and 1 GB of storage, with access to the core Sales, Agreements, and Inventory areas. You can create unlimited quotes and invoices, and exported PDFs do not have a Free-plan watermark.

Free can post invoices. Invoice posting can create the related financial journal processing, but the full Accounting module, including its accounting workspace and reports, remains a paid capability. Invoice posting also requires the appropriate user permission.

Free includes 3 LeadGen credits once. When those included credits are used, you can buy separate LeadGen credit top-ups. Top-up credits are distinct from plan-included credits.

## Pro

Pro is **$14.99 per month** or **$143.90 per year**. It includes 15 LeadGen credits each month. The included allowance renews monthly whether you pay monthly or annually. Any purchased top-up credits remain separate from the monthly plan allowance.

Pro also includes the paid product capabilities described in the app, including Accounting and financial reports. Check the current [product pages](https://lynkacrm.com/product) for the broader product overview.

## Teams

Teams is **$49.99 per month** or **$479.90 per year**. It includes 50 LeadGen credits each month. The included allowance renews monthly whether you pay monthly or annually. Any purchased top-up credits remain separate from the monthly plan allowance.

Teams adds collaboration and access controls for a team. See [how a small team uses Lynka](/teams/run-small-team) for a practical example.

## LeadGen credits by plan

| Plan | Included LeadGen credits | Renewal |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Free Forever | 3 credits once | No recurring allowance |
| Pro | 15 credits | Monthly, including on annual billing |
| Teams | 50 credits | Monthly, including on annual billing |

Free users can purchase additional LeadGen credits. Purchased top-ups are separate from plan-included credits and monthly allowances.

Each LeadGen run shows its credit cost in the product. Failed, canceled, or no-result runs may have their charged credits restored under the run refund rules described in [LeadGen credits and top-ups](/leadgen/credits).

## Other plan access

Plan access is only one part of access. A user may also need the product area enabled and the right role, permission, or record scope for a particular action.
