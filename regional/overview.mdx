Lynka is not documented as if every customer operates in one country.

A business in Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, Lebanon, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, France, Germany, or the UK can share the same core workflow while using different currencies, taxes, timezones, privacy rules, and commercial habits.

The product should keep those local differences configurable without changing what a Lead, Deal, Quote, Invoice, Purchase Order, or Ticket means.

## Set the workspace finance context first

If the business uses Accounting or posted invoices, confirm the workspace base currency and related finance settings before real posting begins.

The base currency is important because reporting can normalize supported transaction values into the workspace's reporting currency when valid FX information exists.

Do not change the base currency casually after real financial history exists.

## Keep transaction currency separate from display preference

A document can use a transaction currency that differs from the workspace base currency where the workflow supports it. That is different from simply changing which currency symbol a user prefers to look at.

[Currency and FX](/regional/currency-fx) explains the distinction.

## Configure tax for the business, not the documentation example

Lynka supports tax configuration and mappings, but the documentation does not assume one country's VAT or sales tax regime.

Your company is responsible for configuring rates and treatment that match its obligations and professional advice.

[Tax across regions](/regional/tax) explains what Lynka controls and what remains a legal or accounting decision.

## Use the correct timezone

Timezone affects what "today" means, when activities are due, how schedules run, and which period a time-sensitive report includes.

A team working between Accra, Beirut, Dubai, and Europe should not assume the browser's local time and the workspace reporting boundary are always the same thing.

## Treat privacy and prospecting rules as regional business requirements

European privacy obligations can be especially relevant when handling personal data, but every region can have its own rules for communications, retention, and customer information.

LeadGen does not convert generated data into automatic permission to contact somebody.

## Mobile is a first-class working environment

For many small businesses, important customer and operational work happens away from a desk. Lynka has dedicated mobile navigation, cards, filters, and full-screen flows instead of simply shrinking desktop tables.

Use the mobile guidance in each feature when the interaction differs.
