Tickets are the working records for customer support issues. Each ticket keeps the customer relationship, issue description, responsibility and support history together.

Open **Support > Tickets** to use the list or Kanban surface. Desktop provides richer table and board controls, while mobile uses compact cards and mobile filters.

## What belongs on a ticket

The create flow can include the company or customer, a related deal, type, category, title, detailed description, impact, priority and assignee.

A related deal can only be selected from deals belonging to the selected company. That keeps support history connected to the correct commercial relationship.

## Ownership and status

Assign the ticket to the person responsible for the next action. Users may need `ticket.assign` permission to change ownership.

Update status as the work changes. A resolved or closed state should represent a genuine support outcome, not simply an attempt to remove the ticket from the queue.

## SLA and automation

Where SLA features are enabled, Lynka can track first response and resolution timing. Automation can surface unassigned tickets, idle work, repeated reopening and SLA risk or breach.
