SLA monitoring turns ticket timing into an operational signal. Instead of relying on someone to remember every deadline, Lynka can surface tickets that are waiting too long or approaching a service commitment.

## First response and resolution are different clocks

**First response** measures how long the customer waits for an initial response. **Resolution** measures how long the issue remains open before completion.

A ticket can meet its first response target and still miss its resolution target, so the two should not be combined into one generic SLA number.

## Built in ticket automation

The active support automation catalog includes:

- Ticket has no assignee
- Ticket idle too long
- Ticket reopened repeatedly
- Ticket SLA at risk
- Ticket SLA breached
- Ticket waiting for first response

The SLA at risk, breached and first response checks can run frequently to support active queue management.

## Reporting

Use **First Reply Time**, **Resolution Time**, **SLA Compliance**, **Incoming Ticket Volume** and **Ticket Backlog by Status** to answer different support questions.

If a ticket is unexpectedly at risk, inspect its current status, response history, resolution timing and SLA configuration before dismissing the warning.
