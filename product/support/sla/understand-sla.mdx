SLA rules turn support timing into an explicit service commitment. Lynka tracks first-response and resolution timing separately because replying to the customer and actually resolving the issue are different events.

## First-response SLA

First-response timing measures how long an eligible ticket waits for the first support response under the configured SLA rules.

The automation catalog includes **Ticket waiting for first response**, **Ticket SLA at risk**, and **Ticket SLA breached**.

## Resolution SLA

Resolution timing measures how long the ticket remains unresolved against the configured resolution target.

A ticket can meet its first-response target and still breach its resolution target later.

## At risk versus breached

**At risk** means the deadline is approaching and the team still has time to act.

**Breached** means the relevant deadline passed without the required response or resolution event.

These are more useful than one generic "late" status because they let a team intervene before the commitment is missed.

## Reports

The active Support report catalog includes:

- First Reply Time
- Resolution Time
- SLA Compliance
- Incoming Ticket Volume
- Ticket Backlog by Status

Ticket reports use the reporting timezone and your workspace's status configuration rather than assuming every workspace has the same labels.

## Assignment and SLA

A ticket can be within SLA but still operationally risky if it has no assignee. Likewise, assigning a ticket does not reset the elapsed SLA clock unless the configured backend rule explicitly does so.
