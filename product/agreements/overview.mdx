Agreements is the commercial document layer between Sales and Accounting. It handles what you offer the customer, what you bill, how the balance is settled, and the document history that supports those decisions.

The current area contains **Quotes**, **Invoices**, and **Customer Contracts**, with document builders, previews, send actions, status history, and templates around those records.

For a high-level view of Lynka Agreements, see the [Agreements product page](https://lynkacrm.com/agreements).

## Quotes

Quotes let you propose products or services, quantities, prices, discounts, tax, currency, validity, and terms before the customer is invoiced.

The server recalculates canonical quote totals from line and tax data so the browser is not the only source of truth for the final amount.

After sending, built in automation can surface quotes waiting for a response, approaching expiry, or expired without a decision.

## Revisions

Negotiation can change price, quantity, scope, or terms. Quote revision history preserves what was previously sent rather than overwriting the customer-facing document sequence.

## Invoices

Invoices record customer billing. Their document status, Accounting posting state, and payment settlement state are separate.

An invoice can be issued but unpaid, posted but unpaid, or later settled after cleared payment. Keeping those states separate is what lets Agreements and Accounting agree without pretending billing, revenue, and cash all happen at the same time.

## Posting and Accounting

Posting creates the ledger consequences of customer billing, such as Accounts Receivable, revenue, and tax payable under the configured posting logic. It requires both plan capability and user permission.

Invoice total can include tax, so billings and Sales Revenue should not be forced to match.

## Fulfillment

For stocked items, an invoice does not prove inventory was fulfilled. Product margin uses posted fulfillment cost so stocked margin can remain incomplete until the physical inventory event is recorded.

## Corrections

Void, refund, credit note, and revision are different correction paths. Choose the one that matches the real event instead of rewriting a settled historical document.

## Reports and automation

Agreements reporting includes Quotes by Status, Quote Acceptance Rate, Quotes Expiring Soon, Quote-to-Invoice Conversion, Invoices Created Trend, Invoices Due Soon, Overdue Invoices, and A/R Aging Summary.

Automation can surface accepted quotes missing invoices, invoices due soon, overdue invoices, partial payments needing follow-up, and quote response or expiry issues.
