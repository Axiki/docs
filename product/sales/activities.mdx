Activities keep the next action attached to the customer record instead of living only in someone's memory or personal calendar.

Open **Sales > Activities** to work with task and activity surfaces. Activities can also be created from lead, deal and company records where the relevant action is available.

## What to record

Use activities for customer facing work and scheduled next steps. Notes are useful for context that should remain on the record but is not itself a scheduled action.

Lynka's calendar surfaces can show scheduled activities in month, week or day views when the backend activity contract returns them successfully.

## Why the next step matters

Open deals without a future activity can be surfaced as **Deal has no next step**. Leads and deals can also be flagged when customer facing activity has gone stale.

A useful activity should tell the next person what needs to happen, when, and against which record.

## Desktop and mobile

Desktop activity surfaces use the shared table, calendar and detail patterns. Mobile uses compact cards and mobile forms while preserving the same record links.
