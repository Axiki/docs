Campaigns give marketing work a durable record inside Lynka. Instead of treating a launch as a name in a spreadsheet, the campaign can stay connected to campaign leads, sales activity and reporting.

Open **Marketing > Campaigns** to create, search and open campaign records.

## What to manage

Keep the campaign name, status, dates and other configured campaign details current. If a campaign has ended, complete its status instead of leaving it active indefinitely.

Lynka includes two built in campaign attention rules:

- **Active campaign has no leads**
- **Campaign ended but still active**

These rules are useful because they catch operational problems that a headline campaign count cannot explain.

## Measuring results

Use **Campaign Performance** to review commercial performance and **Campaign Conversion** to understand conversion behavior. Campaign attribution can use linked touchpoints and opportunities rather than assigning every outcome to the most convenient campaign after the fact.

## Desktop and mobile

Desktop uses campaign list, detail and create views inside the Marketing shell. Mobile converts list content into compact cards and uses mobile create or detail panels.
