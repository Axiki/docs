Tax is part of the invoice and accounting workflow, but tax collected from a customer is not the same thing as business revenue.

## Tax on commercial documents

Quotes and invoices can carry tax lines according to the workspace tax configuration and the document's taxable or tax-exempt state.

The quote calculation engine handles inclusive and exclusive tax separately so a tax-inclusive price is not taxed twice.

## Tax payable

When a taxable invoice is posted, the tax component can post to a tax payable account rather than Sales Revenue.

This is why invoice total and ledger revenue can differ even when the accounting is correct.

## Tax Center

Open **Accounting > Tax** to review the tax surfaces available to your finance role. Financial report access protects the current tax tab, while tax configuration actions can have additional administrative permissions.

## Tax-exempt documents

A tax-exempt quote or invoice should not retain active tax lines that contradict the exemption. The server calculation clears or ignores tax lines according to the supported tax-exempt behavior.

## Audit history

Lynka stores tax audit information for configuration changes. Treat a tax setting change as an accounting policy change that can affect future documents, not as a tool for rewriting historical posted invoices.
