Approvals add a review step before or around a sensitive business action. They are useful when one person prepares work and another person must authorize it.

The current frontend exposes approvals in Settings and the Owner workspace, and team collaboration or approval features depend on the relevant entitlement.

## What approvals are for

An approval request should point to a real operational item that needs review. Examples can include financial, access, or operational actions supported by the configured approval flow.

Approvals do not replace the source record. Approving a request does not mean you should duplicate the transaction in a second place. The source invoice, bill, access change, or other item remains the business record.

## Reviewer access

Owners and admins can receive broader review capability depending on permissions. A reviewer still needs access to enough source context to make the decision safely.

## Approval state and action state

Keep these concepts separate:

- the **approval request** can be pending, approved, rejected, or otherwise resolved by the approval workflow
- the **source record** has its own lifecycle and can still require posting, sending, payment, or another action after approval

This separation prevents an "Approved" badge from being mistaken for a posted financial transaction.

## Plan capability

Team collaboration and approvals are part of the collaboration capability rather than the single-user Pro model. For current commercial packaging, use the [pricing page](https://lynkacrm.com/pricing) instead of relying on a copied price in documentation.
