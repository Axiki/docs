Teams group workspace members for collaboration and access. Lynka's membership model allows a profile to belong to more than one team in the same workspace, so you do not have to duplicate users when someone works across functions.

## Create a team

Open the Teams settings available to owners or admins and create the team with a clear name and purpose. The underlying model supports a team name, description, type, manager, active state, and member records.

Assign a manager when the team should have a clear operational owner.

## Add members

Add active workspace members to the team. Team membership is separate from workspace membership. A person must first belong to the workspace before they can meaningfully participate in team access or reporting.

The canonical membership model supports many teams per profile. That is useful for people such as an operations manager who belongs to both Inventory and a regional sales group.

## Team membership does not transfer record ownership

Adding a person to a team does not automatically make them the owner of every record in that team's scope. Ownership remains a record-level responsibility. Team scope can grant visibility without changing the owner.

## Manager access

Manager behavior depends on the workspace permission and scope configuration. Do not create a fake manager solution by giving every manager unrestricted `view_all` access if the intended policy is team-only visibility.

## Archive or deactivate teams carefully

If a team is no longer used, review the records, scopes, and members that depend on it before disabling or archiving it. Removing the team can change what users see even when their role permissions have not changed.
