## What is Lynka CRM?

Lynka CRM is a comprehensive customer relationship management solution designed for modern businesses. It helps you manage leads, track opportunities, create quotes, process invoices, and collaborate with your team. The platform integrates all the tools you need to manage customer relationships from initial contact to closed deals.

## Core Features

### Lead Management

The lead management system helps you track potential customers from initial contact through to conversion. You can add new leads, update their status, and track their progress through your sales pipeline.

To add a new lead, go to the Leads section and click the "New Lead" button. Fill in the contact information, company details, and any relevant notes. The system will automatically assign a status of "new" but you can update this as the lead progresses.

Lead statuses include:
- New: Recently added leads that haven't been contacted yet
- Contacted: Leads that have been reached out to
- Qualified: Leads that show genuine interest and fit your criteria
- Proposal: Leads that have received a quote or proposal
- Won: Converted leads that became customers
- Lost: Leads that didn't convert

### Opportunity Management

Opportunities represent potential deals that are actively being pursued. Each opportunity is linked to a lead and tracks the potential revenue and probability of closing.

When a lead shows strong interest, you can convert them to an opportunity. The system will automatically create an opportunity record and maintain the connection to the original lead. Opportunities have stages that mirror the sales process:
- New: Initial opportunity created
- Qualified: Opportunity validated with clear requirements
- Proposition: Formal proposal submitted
- Won: Deal closed successfully
- Lost: Opportunity didn't materialize

### Quote Management

The quote system helps you create professional proposals for potential customers. You can build quotes with line items, apply discounts, and track their status.

To create a quote, go to the Quotes section and click "New Quote". Select the associated lead, add line items with descriptions and pricing, and customize the quote as needed. Quotes can be saved as drafts, sent to clients, or converted to invoices once accepted.

Quote statuses include:
- Draft: Quote is being prepared
- Sent: Quote delivered to client
- Viewed: Client has opened the quote
- Accepted: Client has approved the quote
- Rejected: Client declined the quote
- Expired: Quote validity period ended
- Void: Quote cancelled
- Paid: Payment received

### Invoice Management

Invoices handle the billing process for completed work. You can create invoices from accepted quotes or directly for ongoing services.

Invoices can be created from accepted quotes with a single click, or you can create standalone invoices. The system tracks payment status and sends reminders for overdue invoices.

Invoice statuses include:
- Draft: Invoice being prepared
- Posted: Invoice issued to client
- Paid: Full payment received
- Void: Invoice cancelled
- Partially Paid: Partial payment received
- Refunded: Payment refunded to client

### Product Catalog

Manage your products and services in the product catalog. Each product has pricing, descriptions, and other details that can be reused across quotes and invoices.

Add products with names, descriptions, pricing, and categories. You can also specify units of measure, brands, and other relevant details. Products can be added to quotes and invoices to streamline the process.

## Marketing Tools

### WhatsApp Link Generator

Create customized WhatsApp links that allow customers to contact you directly. The tool generates links with pre-filled messages, making it easy for prospects to reach out.

Simply enter your phone number and optional message text. The tool will generate a clickable link that opens WhatsApp with your number and message pre-filled. This is great for marketing materials and social media profiles.

### Email Signature Generator

Build professional email signatures that include your contact information, company details, and social media links. The generator creates HTML signatures that work across email platforms.

Enter your name, title, company, phone number, and other details. You can also add your company logo and social media links. The tool generates a signature that maintains consistent branding across all your emails.

### Business Card QR Generator

Create QR codes that contain your contact information. When scanned, these codes automatically add your contact details to the user's phone.

Input your name, company, phone, email, website, and other contact details. The generator creates a QR code that can be printed on business cards, brochures, or other materials. Scanning the code instantly adds your contact information to the user's contacts.

### UTM Builder

Generate UTM parameters for your marketing links to track campaign performance. This helps you understand which marketing efforts are most effective.

Enter the URL you want to track, campaign name, source, medium, and other parameters. The tool generates a full URL with UTM parameters that can be tracked in your analytics platform.
