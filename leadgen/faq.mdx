## Is LeadGen a separate CRM?

No. LeadGen is part of Lynka. The goal is to move useful prospects into the same Sales workflow where ownership, activities, qualification, Deals, Quotes, Invoices, and reporting already live.

## Does a generated prospect automatically become a Deal?

No. Discovery is separate from qualification. A prospect should normally enter Sales as a Lead while qualification is still open. The Deal is created when the lead passes Lynka's qualification process.

## Can I target by location?

The current backend run contract supports geographic targeting. Use the geography controls exposed by your current LeadGen screen.

## Can I target by industry?

Yes, the current run contract supports industry targeting. The exact list and UI presentation depend on the current LeadGen experience.

## Does LeadGen support enrichment?

The run contract supports enrichment options. Treat enriched information as data to review and verify rather than a guarantee that every field is current.

## Why can I open LeadGen but not press Run?

Free includes LeadGen, but a run still requires remaining credits and permission to run LeadGen. Pro includes 30 credits each month, and Teams includes 100 credits each month. Pro and Teams credits renew monthly whether billing is monthly or annual.

## Do failed runs use my allowance forever?

The current backend finalization logic refunds charged capacity for runs finalized as failed, no results, or canceled when they have not already been refunded.

## How many LeadGen credits are included?

Free includes LeadGen with 3 credits. Pro includes 30 LeadGen credits each month. Teams includes 100 LeadGen credits each month. Pro and Teams credits renew monthly whether billing is monthly or annual.

## What happens when Free credits are used?

LeadGen credit top-ups are not available on Free. Upgrade to Pro to receive 30 new LeadGen credits each month.

## What happens when hosted email reaches a daily or monthly limit?

Hosted-email limits are maximum workspace allowances, not delivery guarantees. The current public implementation does not document a guaranteed queue, retry, reset time, upgrade action, or other specific outcome after a limit is reached. Connected Gmail remains separate and follows Google’s own limits and policies.

## Can my whole team run LeadGen?

Only people with the required access should be able to consume run capacity. You can keep LeadGen limited to an owner or selected sales users while other team members work the Leads and Deals created afterward.

## What if the same company appears twice?

Check existing Sales records and use duplicate review rather than intentionally creating multiple active versions of the same prospect.

## Does LeadGen guarantee that I can legally contact every result?

No. Your business remains responsible for its outreach method, applicable privacy and direct marketing rules, verification, and opt-out handling.
