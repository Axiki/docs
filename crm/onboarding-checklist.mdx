## Success Metrics

### Adoption Metrics
- **User login frequency**: Track how often users log in
- **Feature usage**: Monitor which features are being used
- **Data entry completeness**: Measure how completely data is being entered
- **Task completion**: Track completion of CRM-related tasks

### Performance Metrics
- **Lead response time**: Time from lead creation to first contact
- **Conversion rates**: Track conversion from leads to opportunities to closed deals
- **Sales cycle length**: Average time from lead to closed deal
- **Revenue attribution**: Track revenue generated through CRM activities

### Data Quality Metrics
- **Duplicate rate**: Percentage of duplicate records
- **Data completeness**: Percentage of required fields filled
- **Data accuracy**: Accuracy of information in the system
- **Update frequency**: How often records are updated
