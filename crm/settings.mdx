## Profile Settings

Manage your personal account information, password, and notification preferences. You can update your name, email, phone number, and other personal details.

Access profile settings by clicking your avatar in the top right corner and selecting "Profile Settings". Here you can update your personal information, change your password, and manage notification preferences.

## Display Settings

Customize the appearance and behavior of the CRM interface. You can change themes, adjust font sizes, and configure other display preferences.

Display settings include options for light or dark mode, font scaling, and interface density. You can also configure which columns appear in data tables and how information is displayed.

## Security Settings

Manage authentication, password policies, and security preferences. Two-factor authentication and other security measures can be configured here.

Security settings allow you to enable two-factor authentication, manage active sessions, and configure password requirements. You can also review recent login activity and security events.

## Organization Settings

Configure company-wide settings including business information, branding, and operational preferences.

Organization settings include company name, address, tax information, and other business details. You can also configure default currencies, time zones, and other operational settings.

## Pipeline Stages

Customize the stages that leads and opportunities move through in your sales process. You can add, remove, or rename stages to match your workflow.

Pipeline stages can be configured for both leads and opportunities. Each stage can have a name, position in the sequence, and active status. You can also customize the labels that appear in the interface.

## Finance and Currency

Set up financial settings including base currency, tax rates, and payment terms. These settings affect how financial data is displayed and calculated.

Finance settings include your base currency, tax calculation methods, and payment terms. You can also configure which currencies you work with and how exchange rates are handled.

## Invoicing Configuration

Customize invoice templates, numbering, and other billing preferences. These settings control how invoices appear and behave.

Invoicing settings include template selection, numbering sequences, due dates, and payment terms. You can also configure automatic reminders for overdue invoices and customize the appearance of invoices.
