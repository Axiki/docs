## Overview

Invoices handle the billing process for completed work. You can create invoices from accepted quotes or directly for ongoing services.

## Creating Invoices

Invoices can be created from accepted quotes with a single click, or you can create standalone invoices. The system tracks payment status and sends reminders for overdue invoices.

### From Quotes
When a quote is accepted, you can convert it to an invoice. The system automatically transfers:
- All line items
- Pricing and quantities
- Tax calculations
- Customer information

### Standalone Invoices
Create invoices directly without a quote:
- Select the associated lead
- Add line items manually
- Apply taxes and discounts
- Set payment terms

## Invoice Details

Each invoice includes:

- Invoice number: Unique identifier
- Issue date: When the invoice was created
- Due date: When payment is expected
- Recipient: Customer information
- Line items: Detailed breakdown of charges
- Totals: Subtotal, tax, and final amount
- Payment status: Current payment status

## Invoice Statuses

Invoice statuses include:
- Draft: Invoice being prepared
- Posted: Invoice issued to client
- Paid: Full payment received
- Void: Invoice cancelled
- Partially Paid: Partial payment received
- Refunded: Payment refunded to client

## Payment Tracking

Track payments against invoices:
- Record payment amounts
- Specify payment methods
- Add payment notes
- Track partial payments
- Process refunds

## Overdue Invoices

The system helps manage overdue invoices:
- Automatic reminders
- Overdue status indicators
- Collections tracking
- Late fee calculations

## PDF Generation

Generate professional PDF invoices:
- Customizable templates
- Company branding
- Automatic delivery
- Download for record keeping
