## Overview

The invoice management system handles billing for completed work. You can create invoices from accepted quotes or directly for ongoing services. The system tracks payment status and sends reminders for overdue invoices, helping you maintain healthy cash flow.

## Creating Invoices

### From Quotes

Convert accepted quotes to invoices:

<Steps>
<Step title="Select accepted quote**:
  Choose the quote that has been approved by the customer.
</Step>

<Step title="Click convert to invoice**:
  Select "Convert to Invoice" from the quote actions.
</Step>

<Step title="Review invoice details**:
  Verify the information transferred from the quote.
</Step>

<Step title="Set invoice terms**:
  Configure due date, payment terms, and other invoice-specific details.
</Step>

<Step title="Save invoice**:
  Create the invoice record linked to the original quote.
</Step>
</Steps>

#### Benefits of Quote-to-Invoice Conversion
<Tip>
  **Data preservation**: All quote information transfers to the invoice, maintaining consistency and reducing data entry.
</Tip>

- **Line items**: All quote line items transfer to invoice
- **Customer information**: Contact details and company information carry over
- **Pricing**: All pricing remains consistent from quote to invoice
- **Tax calculations**: Tax rates and calculations transfer accurately
- **History**: Complete history maintained from quote to invoice

### Direct Creation

Create invoices without converting from a quote:

<Steps>
<Step title="Navigate to Invoices**:
  Go to the Invoices section in your CRM.
</Step>

<Step title="Click New Invoice**:
  Select the "New Invoice" button.
</Step>

<Step title="Select associated lead**:
  Choose the customer the invoice is for.
</Step>

<Step title="Add invoice details**:
  Enter invoice number, due date, and other information.
</Step>

<Step title="Add line items**:
  Include products/services with descriptions and pricing.
</Step>

<Step title="Save invoice**:
  Create the invoice record.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Invoice Statuses

### Status Definitions

#### Draft
Invoice is being prepared and hasn't been issued to the customer yet. You can continue editing the invoice and make changes as needed.

#### Posted
Invoice has been issued to the customer and is awaiting payment. The invoice is no longer editable but can be viewed and tracked.

#### Paid
Full payment has been received for the invoice. The invoice is complete and closed.

#### Void
Invoice has been cancelled and is no longer valid. Voided invoices are kept for record keeping but don't affect financial reporting.

#### Partially Paid
Partial payment has been received, but the full amount is not yet paid. The remaining balance continues to be tracked.

#### Refunded
Payment has been refunded to the customer. The invoice shows as refunded and the amount is returned to the customer.

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### Payment Not Recording
<Steps>
<Step title="Verify payment details**:
  Check that payment amount and date are correct.
</Step>

<Step title="Confirm method**:
  Verify the payment method is correctly selected.
</Step>

<Step title="Check invoice status**:
  Ensure the invoice is in a state that accepts payments.
</Step>

<Step title="Try again**:
  Attempt to record the payment again.
</Step>

<Step title="Contact support**:
  If issues persist, contact support for assistance.
</Step>
</Steps>

#### Invoice Calculation Errors
- **Review line items**: Check all line items and calculations for accuracy
- **Verify product pricing**: Ensure product catalog pricing is current
- **Check discounts**: Verify discount calculations are correct
- **Tax calculations**: Confirm tax rates are applied correctly
- **Currency settings**: Check that currency settings are correct

#### Template Issues
- **Check template settings**: Verify template configuration is correct
- **Review branding elements**: Ensure logos and colors are properly set
- **Test with sample**: Test templates with sample invoices before using
- **Update branding**: Refresh branding elements if they're not displaying
- **Clear cache**: Clear browser cache if templates aren't updating

#### Overdue Invoice Management
- **Review reminder settings**: Check that automated reminders are configured
- **Verify due dates**: Confirm due dates are set correctly
- **Check customer communication**: Ensure customers received invoices
- **Payment processing**: Verify payment processing is working correctly
- **Follow-up process**: Implement manual follow-up for persistent overdue invoices

<Note>
  **Cash flow management**: Monitor invoice aging and payment patterns to maintain healthy cash flow.
