## Data Management Best Practices

### Lead Quality Management

#### Lead Validation Process
<Tip>
  **Verify before adding**: Always validate contact information before adding leads to ensure quality.
</Tip>

<Steps>
<Step title="Email verification**:
  Use email verification tools to ensure deliverability.
</Step>

<Step title="Phone validation**:
  Verify phone numbers are valid and reachable.
</Step>

<Step title="Company verification**:
  Confirm company existence and relevance.
</Step>

<Step title="Social validation**:
  Check social media profiles for authenticity.
</Step>

<Step title="Website verification**:
  Verify company websites are active and legitimate.
</Step>
</Steps>

#### Lead Enrichment Strategy
- **Complete profiles**: Fill in all available information about leads
- **Social media links**: Include LinkedIn, Twitter, and other social profiles
- **Company details**: Add industry, size, and other company information
- **Decision maker identification**: Identify key decision makers
- **Contact preferences**: Note preferred communication methods
