## Overview

The tasks and activities system helps you stay organized and track important interactions with customers. Use tasks for specific actions and activities for customer interactions. This system ensures nothing falls through the cracks and maintains a complete history of customer engagement.

## Task Management

### Creating Tasks

#### New Task Creation Process
<Steps>
<Step title="Navigate to Tasks**:
  Go to the Tasks section in your CRM.
</Step>

<Step title="Click New Task**:
  Select the "New Task" button.
</Step>

<Step title="Enter task details**:
  Add title, description, and due date.
</Step>

<Step title="Set priority and owner**:
  Assign priority level and responsible person.
</Step>

<Step title="Link to entity**:
  Optionally link to a lead, opportunity, or other entity.
</Step>

<Step title="Save task**:
  Create the task record.
</Step>
</Steps>

#### Task Details

##### Required Information
- **Title**: Descriptive name for the task
- **Due Date**: When the task should be completed

##### Optional Information
- **Description**: Detailed information about the task
- **Priority**: Priority level (Low, Medium, High, Urgent)
- **Owner**: Person responsible for completing the task
- **Status**: Current status (Pending, In Progress, Completed, Cancelled)
- **Category**: Task category for organization
- **Estimated Time**: Time needed to complete the task
- **Linked Entity**: Link to related lead, opportunity, etc.

### Task Priorities

#### Priority Levels
- **Low**: Can be completed when convenient
- **Medium**: Should be completed soon
- **High**: Requires prompt attention
- **Urgent**: Requires immediate attention

### Task Statuses

- **Pending**: Task is waiting to be started
- **In Progress**: Task is actively being worked on
- **Completed**: Task has been finished
- **Cancelled**: Task is no longer needed

## Activity Tracking

### Creating Activities

#### Activity Creation Process
<Steps>
<Step title="Navigate to Activities**:
  Go to the Activities section or create from an entity page.
</Step>

<Step title="Click New Activity**:
  Select the "New Activity" button.
</Step>

<Step title="Select activity type**:
  Choose from call, meeting, email, or other activity types.
</Step>

<Step title="Enter activity details**:
  Add title, description, and date/time.
</Step>

<Step title="Link to entity**:
  Connect to relevant lead, opportunity, or other entity.
</Step>

<Step title="Save activity**:
  Create the activity record.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Activity Types

#### Call Activities
- **Phone number**: Contact phone number
- **Call duration**: Length of the call
- **Call outcome**: Result of the call
- **Follow-up needed**: Whether follow-up is required
- **Recording**: Link to call recording if available

#### Meeting Activities
- **Meeting type**: In-person, virtual, or hybrid
- **Attendees**: List of meeting participants
- **Location**: Meeting location or virtual link
- **Duration**: Expected meeting length
- **Agenda**: Meeting agenda items
- **Notes**: Meeting notes and action items

#### Email Activities
- **Subject**: Email subject line
- **Recipients**: Email recipients
- **Email type**: Outgoing or incoming
- **Status**: Sent, opened, replied, etc.
- **Attachments**: Any attached files
- **Template used**: Template if applicable

#### Other Activities
- **Note**: General activity notes
- **Task**: Linked task completion
- **Follow-up**: Scheduled follow-up activities
- **Reminder**: Time-based reminders
- **Document**: Document creation or sharing
