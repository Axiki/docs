## Overview

Tasks and activities help you stay organized and track important interactions with customers. Use tasks for specific actions and activities for customer interactions.

## Task Management

### Creating Tasks
Create tasks to track specific actions:
- Set due dates and priorities
- Assign to team members
- Add detailed descriptions
- Link to specific leads or opportunities

### Task Priorities
Tasks can have different priority levels:
- Low: Can be completed when convenient
- Medium: Should be completed soon
- High: Requires immediate attention
- Urgent: Critical task requiring immediate action

### Task Statuses
Track task progress:
- Pending: Task is waiting to be started
- In Progress: Task is actively being worked on
- Completed: Task has been finished
- Cancelled: Task is no longer needed

### Assigning Tasks
- Assign to yourself or team members
- Set due dates for accountability
- Add notes for context
- Receive notifications for assigned tasks

## Activity Tracking

### Types of Activities
Track different types of customer interactions:
- Calls: Phone conversations
- Emails: Email correspondence
- Meetings: In-person or virtual meetings
- Follow-ups: Follow-up actions
- Tasks: Specific action items

### Recording Activities
Log activities to maintain a complete history:
- Date and time of the interaction
- Type of activity
- Duration (for calls and meetings)
- Participants involved
- Notes about the interaction
- Outcome or next steps

### Activity Timeline
View all interactions with a customer in chronological order:
- Complete history of touchpoints
- Track relationship development
- Identify patterns in customer behavior
- Ensure consistent follow-up

## Notifications and Reminders

### Task Reminders
Receive notifications for:
- Upcoming due dates
- Overdue tasks
- Assigned tasks
- Task status changes

### Activity Alerts
Stay informed about:
- Important customer interactions
- Follow-up requirements
- Meeting reminders
- Deadline notifications

## Best Practices

### Task Management
- Break large projects into smaller tasks
- Set realistic deadlines
- Prioritize based on business impact
- Regularly review and update task lists

### Activity Tracking
- Record interactions promptly
- Include relevant details
- Note next steps and follow-up items
- Maintain consistent activity logging
