## Step 1: Account Setup

### Initial Configuration
1. **Verify your email address** by clicking the link in the verification email
2. **Complete your profile** with your name, title, and contact information
3. **Set your timezone and language preferences**
4. **Choose your plan features** based on your business needs

### Company Information
- Company name and legal entity
- Business address and contact information
- Industry and company size
- Fiscal year and currency settings
- Logo and branding elements

## Step 3: Data Migration

### Importing Existing Data
You can import your existing customer data to get started quickly:

<Steps>
<Step title="Prepare your data">
  Format your data in CSV or Excel format with appropriate column headers.
</Step>

<Step title="Map fields">
  Match your data columns to CRM fields during the import process.
</Step>

<Step title="Preview import">
  Review the data mapping and preview how records will be created.
</Step>

<Step title="Execute import">
  Start the import process and monitor for any errors or issues.
</Step>

<Step title="Verify data">
  Check a sample of imported records to ensure accuracy.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Supported Import Types
- **Contacts/Leads**: Names, emails, phone numbers, company information
- **Opportunities**: Deal information, amounts, probabilities, stages
- **Products/Services**: Catalog items with pricing and descriptions
- **Activities**: Historical interactions and communications

<Note>
  **Data Quality**: Clean your data before importing to ensure accuracy. Remove duplicates and standardize formats.
</Note>

## Best Practices

<Tip>
  **Start Simple**: Begin with basic configuration and gradually add complexity as you become more familiar with the system.
</Tip>

<Info>
  **Regular Reviews**: Schedule monthly reviews of your CRM configuration to ensure it continues to meet your business needs.
</Info>

<Warning>
  **Data Hygiene**: Regularly clean and update your data to maintain accuracy and effectiveness.
</Warning>

### Common Onboarding Mistakes to Avoid

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion icon="triangle-exclamation" title="Over-customizing too early">
  <p>Don't try to customize everything on day one. Start with default settings and customize based on actual usage patterns.</p>
</Accordion>

<Accordion icon="triangle-exclamation" title="Skipping team training">
  <p>Ensure all team members receive proper training before going live. Rushing into using the system without proper training leads to poor adoption.</p>
</Accordion>

<Accordion icon="triangle-exclamation" title="Importing unclean data">
  <p>Don't import messy data without cleaning it first. Poor data quality will undermine the value of your CRM.</p>
</Accordion>

<Accordion icon="triangle-exclamation" title="Not establishing processes">
  <p>Define your sales processes before configuring the CRM. The system should support your processes, not define them.</p>
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Going Live

### Pre-Launch Checklist
- [ ] All team members trained on system
- [ ] Data imported and verified
- [ ] Processes documented and communicated
- [ ] Backup and security measures in place
- [ ] Support contacts identified
- [ ] Success metrics defined

### Launch Strategy
<Steps>
<Step title="Soft launch">
  Start with a small group of power users to identify any issues.
</Step>

<Step title="Gradual rollout">
  Expand to additional team members over a 1-2 week period.
</Step>

<Step title="Monitor adoption">
  Track usage metrics and address any challenges quickly.
</Step>

<Step title="Gather feedback">
  Collect feedback from users and make necessary adjustments.
</Step>

<Step title="Optimize">
  Continuously improve based on usage patterns and feedback.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Measuring Success

Track these key metrics to measure your CRM success:

- **User Adoption**: Percentage of team members actively using the system
- **Data Completeness**: Percentage of required fields filled out
- **Activity Levels**: Number of calls, emails, and tasks logged
- **Pipeline Velocity**: Average time from lead to opportunity to close
- **Conversion Rates**: Percentage of leads that convert to opportunities and deals
- **ROI**: Time saved and revenue generated through CRM usage

<Tip>
  **90-Day Goal**: Aim for 80% user adoption and 90% data completeness within your first 90 days for optimal success.
</Tip>
