## Getting Started

Welcome to Lynka CRM! This guide will walk you through the complete onboarding process to get you up and running quickly and efficiently.

### Initial Setup

<Steps>
<Step title="Create your account**:
  Sign up using your email or Google account and verify your email address.
</Step>

<Step title="Complete profile setup**:
  Add your name, company information, and contact details.
</Step>

<Step title="Configure basic settings**:
  Set your timezone, currency, and other basic preferences.
</Step>

<Step title="Set up security**:
  Enable two-factor authentication and configure security settings.
</Step>

<Step title="Invite team members**:
  Add your team members and assign appropriate roles.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Company Information

#### Required Information
- **Company name**: Your business name
- **Address**: Business address information
- **Timezone**: Your local timezone
- **Currency**: Base currency for all financial data
- **Industry**: Your business industry
- **Company size**: Number of employees

#### Optional Information
- **Company website**: Your business website
- **Phone number**: Business phone number
- **Tax information**: VAT or tax ID numbers
- **Branding**: Logo and brand colors
- **Business hours**: Operating hours information

## Data Migration

### Importing Existing Data

#### Pre-Migration Checklist
<Steps>
<Step title="Audit existing data**:
  Review your current data for quality and completeness.
</Step>

<Step title="Clean data**:
  Remove duplicates and correct any errors in your data.
</Step>

<Step title="Map fields**:
  Determine how your current fields map to CRM fields.
</Step>

<Step title="Test with sample**:
  Test the migration process with a small sample of data first.
</Step>

<Step title="Plan timeline**:
  Schedule the migration during low-usage periods.
</Step>

<Step title="Notify stakeholders**:
  Inform team members about the migration timeline.
</Step>
</Steps>

#### Migration Process
- **Full backup**: Create a complete backup of your current data
- **Execute migration**: Run the data migration process
- **Verify integrity**: Check that all data was migrated correctly
- **Test functionality**: Test all features with the migrated data
- **Resolve issues**: Address any problems that arise
- **Update documentation**: Update any documentation with new processes

### Data Sources Supported
- **CRM exports**: Data from other CRM systems
- **Spreadsheets**: Excel, CSV, and Google Sheets
- **Contact managers**: Outlook, Gmail, and other contact managers
- **Business cards**: Business card scanner integration
- **Social media**: LinkedIn and other social media connections
