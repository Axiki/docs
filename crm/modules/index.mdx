## Module Interconnections

The modules in Lynka CRM are designed to work together seamlessly:

1. **Leads** become **Opportunities** when they show interest
2. **Opportunities** lead to **Quotes** when proposals are made
3. **Quotes** convert to **Invoices** when accepted
4. **Products** are used in both **Quotes** and **Invoices**
5. **Tasks** and **Activities** track progress across all modules

## Getting Started with Each Module

### Lead Management
Start by importing or adding your existing leads to the system. This is the foundation of your CRM where all potential customers begin their journey.

### Opportunity Tracking
As leads show interest, convert them to opportunities to track them through your sales pipeline with specific values and probabilities.

### Quote Creation
When opportunities are ready for proposals, create quotes with your products and services to present to potential customers.

### Invoice Processing
Once quotes are accepted, convert them to invoices for billing and payment tracking.

### Product Catalog
Maintain your product and service catalog to streamline the quoting process and ensure consistent pricing.

## Module Permissions

Each module can have different permission levels:
- **View Only**: Read access to data
- **Edit**: Ability to modify records
- **Create**: Ability to create new records
- **Delete**: Ability to remove records
- **Full Access**: Complete administrative control

## Data Flow Between Modules

### Lead to Opportunity
- Automatic data transfer when converting leads
- Maintains connection between related records
- Preserves all lead history and notes

### Opportunity to Quote
- Create quotes directly from opportunities
- Carry over relevant opportunity information
- Track quote acceptance back to original opportunity

### Quote to Invoice
- Convert accepted quotes to invoices with one click
- Maintain all quote line items and pricing
- Track payment status and history

### Product Integration
- Products available across all modules
- Consistent pricing and descriptions
- Inventory tracking where applicable

<Tip>
  **Start simple**: Begin with lead management and gradually incorporate other modules as you become comfortable with the system.
</Tip>

<Info>
  **Data consistency**: Information entered in one module is available in related modules for consistency.
</Info>

<Warning>
  **Module dependencies**: Some modules depend on others for full functionality. Set up in the recommended order for best results.
</Warning>

## Module Customization

Each module can be customized to match your business processes:
- **Field customization**: Add custom fields to capture business-specific information
- **Workflow automation**: Set up automated processes between modules
- **Permission settings**: Configure access levels for different team members
- **Notification rules**: Set up alerts for important module events
- **Integration points**: Connect modules with external systems
