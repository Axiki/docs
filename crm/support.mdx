## Customer Support

Contact our support team for assistance with technical issues or questions about using the CRM.

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion icon="envelope" title="Email Support">
  Send us an email at support@lynka-crm.com. Our support team typically responds within 24 hours during business days.
</Accordion>

<Accordion icon="comments" title="Live Chat">
  Use the live chat feature in the bottom right corner of the CRM interface. Available Monday-Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM EST.
</Accordion>

<Accordion icon="phone" title="Phone Support">
  Call our support line at +1 (800) 555-1234. Premium support packages include dedicated phone support.

## Submitting Feedback

We value your feedback and continuously improve the CRM based on user input:

<Steps>
<Step title="In-app Feedback">
  Use the feedback button in the CRM interface to submit suggestions or report issues.
</Step>

<Step title="Feature Requests">
  Submit feature requests through our public roadmap at roadmap.lynka-crm.com.
</Step>

<Step title="Bug Reports">
  Report bugs through the support portal with detailed reproduction steps.
</Step>

<Step title="User Interviews">
  Participate in user interviews to help shape future development.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  **Pro tip**: Check the status of known issues and planned features on our public roadmap before submitting feedback.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Premium support customers receive dedicated account management and priority support.
</Note>
