## Team Members

Invite and manage team members with different roles and permissions. Each team member has their own login and can access the CRM based on their assigned permissions.

To add team members, go to Settings > Team Members and click "Invite Member". Enter their email address and select their role. They will receive an invitation to join the team.

## Roles and Permissions

Configure what each team member can access and do within the CRM. Permissions can be granular, allowing different levels of access to different parts of the system.

Roles include administrator, sales manager, sales representative, and other custom roles. Each role can have different permissions for viewing, editing, and deleting data. You can also configure field-level permissions for sensitive information.
