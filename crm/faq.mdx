## Account & Setup

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion icon="user" title="How do I create an account?">
  You can create an account by visiting our website and clicking the "Sign Up" button. You can sign up using your email address or sign in with Google. After signing up, you'll need to verify your email address before accessing the CRM.
</Accordion>

<Accordion icon="key" title="How do I reset my password?">
  If you've forgotten your password, click the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. Enter your email address and we'll send you a password reset link. The link will expire after 24 hours for security purposes.
</Accordion>

<Accordion icon="users" title="How many team members can I add?">
  The number of team members depends on your subscription plan. The free plan includes 1 user, the Pro plan includes up to 5 users, and the Teams plan supports unlimited users. You can upgrade your plan at any time to add more team members.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Lead Management

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion icon="user-plus" title="How do I add leads to the CRM?">
  You can add leads in several ways:
  - Manually through the "New Lead" button in the Leads section
  - Import from CSV or Excel files
  - Through API integration
  - Using the lead generation tools built into the CRM
</Accordion>

<Accordion icon="sync" title="How do I prevent duplicate leads?">
  The CRM has built-in duplicate detection that runs automatically when adding new leads. It checks for matching email addresses, phone numbers, and company names. You can also manually search for existing leads before adding new ones.
</Accordion>

<Accordion icon="filter" title="Can I filter and search leads?">
  Yes, you can filter leads by status, source, date, and other criteria. You can also search for specific leads using keywords. Advanced filtering options allow you to create custom views of your lead data.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Opportunities & Sales

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion icon="chart-line" title="How do opportunities relate to leads?">
  Opportunities are created from qualified leads that show strong interest in your products or services. Each opportunity is linked to its originating lead, allowing you to track the entire sales process from initial contact to closed deal.
</Accordion>

<Accordion icon="file-invoice-dollar" title="How do I convert quotes to invoices?">
  Once a quote is accepted by a customer, you can convert it to an invoice with a single click. The system automatically transfers all line items, pricing, and customer information from the quote to the invoice.
</Accordion>

<Accordion icon="dollar-sign" title="Can I track sales performance?">
  Yes, the CRM includes comprehensive reporting tools that allow you to track sales performance, conversion rates, average deal size, sales cycle length, and other key metrics. You can create custom reports and schedule them to be delivered automatically.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Billing & Payments

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion icon="credit-card" title="What payment methods do you accept?">
  We accept all major credit cards including Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover. For annual subscriptions, we also accept wire transfers and ACH payments. Enterprise customers can request custom billing arrangements.
</Accordion>

<Accordion icon="rotate" title="Can I change my subscription plan?">
  Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your subscription plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately, and you'll be charged or credited appropriately based on the remaining time in your billing cycle.
</Accordion>

<Accordion icon="receipt" title="How do I access invoices for my subscription?">
  You can view and download invoices for your subscription in the Billing section of your account settings. Invoices are also emailed to the billing contact address on file.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Technical Support

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion icon="server" title="What browsers are supported?">
  Lynka CRM works with all modern browsers including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. We recommend using the latest version of your preferred browser for the best experience. The CRM is fully responsive and works on mobile devices.
</Accordion>

<Accordion icon="database" title="How is my data secured?">
  We take data security seriously. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We use industry-standard security measures including two-factor authentication, regular security audits, and compliance with GDPR and other privacy regulations.
</Accordion>

<Accordion icon="clock" title="How often is data backed up?">
  Your data is backed up multiple times per day. We maintain backups for 30 days and can restore data from any point within that period. For enterprise customers, we can provide longer backup retention periods.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Integrations

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion icon="plug" title="What integrations are available?">
  Lynka CRM integrates with popular tools including Gmail, Outlook, Slack, Salesforce, QuickBooks, and many others. We also provide a comprehensive API for custom integrations. Check our integration marketplace for the latest list of available integrations.
</Accordion>

<Accordion icon="code" title="How do I connect to the API?">
  API access is available on all paid plans. You can generate API keys in your account settings under Security. We provide comprehensive API documentation and code examples to help you get started with custom integrations.
</Accordion>

<Accordion icon="download" title="Can I export my data?">
  Yes, you can export your data in various formats including CSV, Excel, and JSON. You can export leads, opportunities, quotes, invoices, and other data. For large exports, we recommend using the API for better performance.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
