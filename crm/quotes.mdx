## Overview

The quote management system helps you create professional proposals for potential customers. You can build quotes with line items, apply discounts, and track their status. Quotes can be converted to invoices once accepted, creating a seamless flow from proposal to billing.

## Creating Quotes

### From Opportunities

Create quotes from qualified opportunities:

<Steps>
<Step title="Select qualified opportunity**:
  Choose an opportunity that's ready for a proposal.
</Step>

<Step title="Click create quote**:
  Select "Create Quote" from the opportunity actions.
</Step>

<Step title="Configure quote details**:
  Set title, validity period, and other quote-specific information.
</Step>

<Step title="Add line items**:
  Include products/services with descriptions and pricing.
</Step>

<Step title="Review and send**:
  Verify the quote and send to the customer.
</Step>
</Steps>

#### Benefits of Opportunity-Based Quotes
<Tip>
  **Context preservation**: Creating quotes from opportunities maintains all relevant context and information.
</Tip>

- **Contact information**: Opportunity contact details automatically populate
- **Company details**: Company information carries over from opportunity
- **Relationship context**: Complete interaction history preserved
- **Value alignment**: Quote value aligns with opportunity expectations
- **Timeline consistency**: Quote timeline matches opportunity timeline

### Direct Creation

Create quotes without linking to an opportunity:

<Steps>
<Step title="Navigate to Quotes**:
  Go to the Quotes section in your CRM.
</Step>

<Step title="Click New Quote**:
  Select the "New Quote" button.
</Step>

<Step title="Select associated lead**:
  Choose the lead the quote is for.
</Step>

<Step title="Add quote details**:
  Enter title, validity period, and other information.
</Step>

<Step title="Add line items**:
  Include products/services with pricing.
</Step>

<Step title="Save quote**:
  Create the quote record.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Quote Statuses

### Status Definitions

#### Draft
Quote is being prepared and hasn't been sent to the customer yet. You can continue editing the quote and make changes as needed.

#### Sent
Quote has been delivered to the customer. The quote is no longer editable but can be viewed and tracked.

#### Viewed
Customer has opened and viewed the quote. This indicates interest and provides timing information for follow-up.

#### Accepted
Customer has approved the quote and agreed to proceed. This quote can be converted to an invoice.

#### Rejected
Customer has declined the quote. Document the reason for future reference and potential re-engagement strategies.

#### Expired
Quote validity period has ended without acceptance. The quote is no longer valid.

#### Void
Quote has been cancelled and is no longer valid.

#### Paid
Payment has been received for the quote (usually after conversion to invoice).

### Status Management

#### Automatic Updates
- **View tracking**: Automatically updates to "Viewed" when customer opens quote
- **Expiration**: Automatically updates to "Expired" when validity period ends
- **Payment tracking**: Updates to "Paid" when payment is received

#### Manual Updates
- **Status changes**: Manually update status based on customer communication
- **Reason documentation**: Document reasons for status changes
- **Follow-up actions**: Trigger follow-up actions based on status changes

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### Quote Not Converting to Invoice
<Steps>
<Step title="Verify status**:
  Ensure the quote status is "Accepted".
</Step>

<Step title="Check permissions**:
  Verify you have permission to convert quotes.
</Step>

<Step title="Review data**:
  Check that all required invoice fields are populated.
</Step>

<Step title="Try again**:
  Attempt the conversion again.
</Step>

<Step title="Contact support**:
  If issues persist, contact support for assistance.
</Step>
</Steps>

#### Pricing Errors
- **Review line items**: Check all line items and calculations for accuracy
- **Verify product pricing**: Ensure product catalog pricing is current
- **Check discounts**: Verify discount calculations are correct
- **Tax calculations**: Confirm tax rates are applied correctly
- **Currency settings**: Check that currency settings are correct

#### Template Issues
- **Check template settings**: Verify template configuration is correct
- **Review branding elements**: Ensure logos and colors are properly set
- **Test with sample**: Test templates with sample quotes before using
- **Update branding**: Refresh branding elements if they're not displaying
- **Clear cache**: Clear browser cache if templates aren't updating

<Note>
  **Quote follow-up**: Implement a follow-up process for quotes that have been sent but not yet accepted or rejected.
</Note>

<Warning>
  **Validity periods**: Set appropriate validity periods to ensure pricing accuracy and avoid disputes.
</Warning>
