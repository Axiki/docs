## Overview

The quote system helps you create professional proposals for potential customers. You can build quotes with line items, apply discounts, and track their status.

## Creating Quotes

To create a quote, go to the Quotes section and click "New Quote". Select the associated lead, add line items with descriptions and pricing, and customize the quote as needed. Quotes can be saved as drafts, sent to clients, or converted to invoices once accepted.

## Quote Components

### Quote Header
- Title: Descriptive name for the quote
- Quote number: Unique identifier (auto-generated or custom)
- Valid until: Expiration date for the quote
- Associated lead: Link to the lead the quote is for

### Line Items
- Product/service: Item being quoted
- Description: Detailed description of the item
- Quantity: Number of units
- Unit price: Price per unit
- Cost: Internal cost (for profit calculations)
- Tax rate: Tax percentage to apply

### Adjustments
- Discounts: Reductions from the total
- Fees: Additional charges to add to the total
- Taxes: Tax calculations applied to the quote

## Quote Statuses

Quote statuses include:
- Draft: Quote is being prepared
- Sent: Quote delivered to client
- Viewed: Client has opened the quote
- Accepted: Client has approved the quote
- Rejected: Client declined the quote
- Expired: Quote validity period ended
- Void: Quote cancelled
- Paid: Payment received

## Templates

Use quote templates to standardize your proposals:
- Create reusable layouts
- Include company branding
- Add standard terms and conditions
- Save time on quote creation

## Converting to Invoices

Once a quote is accepted, you can convert it to an invoice with a single click. The system preserves all line items and pricing while creating a billable invoice.
