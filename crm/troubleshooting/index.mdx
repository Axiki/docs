## Common Issues

### Login Problems

#### I can't log in to my account
<Steps>
<Step title="Check your credentials">
  Make sure you're using the correct email and password. Passwords are case-sensitive.
</Step>

<Step title="Reset your password">
  If you've forgotten your password, use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page to reset it.
</Step>

<Step title="Check your email verification">
  If you just created an account, make sure you've verified your email address by clicking the link in the verification email.
</Step>

<Step title="Clear your browser cache">
  Sometimes clearing your browser's cache and cookies can resolve login issues.
</Step>

<Step title="Try a different browser">
  If you're still having issues, try logging in with a different browser or in an incognito/private window.
</Step>
</Steps>

#### Two-factor authentication issues
If you're having trouble with two-factor authentication:

<Steps>
<Step title="Check your authenticator app">
  Ensure your authenticator app's time is synchronized with your device.
</Step>

<Step title="Use backup codes">
  If you can't access your authenticator app, use one of your backup codes.
</Step>

<Step title="Contact support">
  If you've lost access to both your authenticator app and backup codes, contact support for assistance.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Data Issues

#### Leads are not appearing in my list
<TroubleshootingSteps>
<TroubleshootingStep title="Check your filters">
  Look at the active filters in your leads view. You might have filters applied that are hiding leads.
</TroubleshootingStep>

<TroubleshootingStep title="Verify permissions">
  Ensure you have the necessary permissions to view the leads. If you're on a team account, check if leads are assigned to other team members.
</TroubleshootingStep>

<TroubleshootingStep title="Check lead status">
  Some views only show leads with specific statuses. Verify you're not filtering by status.
</TroubleshootingStep>

<TroubleshootingStep title="Refresh the page">
  Try refreshing your browser to ensure you have the latest data.
</TroubleshootingStep>
</TroubleshootingSteps>

#### Data is not saving
<Steps>
<Step title="Check your internet connection">
  Ensure you have a stable internet connection.
</Step>

<Step title="Look for error messages">
  Check if there are any error messages displayed when you try to save.
</Step>

<Step title="Validate required fields">
  Make sure all required fields are filled out correctly.
</Step>

<Step title="Try again">
  Sometimes a temporary issue can cause saving to fail. Try saving again after a few seconds.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Email and Notifications

#### I'm not receiving email notifications
<Steps>
<Step title="Check notification settings">
  Go to Settings > Notifications and verify that email notifications are enabled for the events you want to receive.
</Step>

<Step title="Check your spam folder">
  Sometimes email notifications end up in your spam or junk folder.
</Step>

<Step title="Verify your email address">
  Ensure your email address is correct in your profile settings.
</Step>

<Step title="Check email provider filters">
  Some email providers have aggressive filtering that might block notifications.
</Step>

<Step title="Whitelist our domain">
  Add our email domain to your email provider's whitelist.
</Step>
</Steps>

#### Email templates are not working
<Steps>
<Step title="Check template syntax">
  Verify that any merge fields in your template are correctly formatted.
</Step>

<Step title="Test with a single recipient">
  Try sending the email to yourself first to test the template.
</Step>

<Step title="Check for blocked content">
  Some email providers block certain types of content or links.
</Step>

<Step title="Verify sender settings">
  Ensure your email sending settings are properly configured.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Performance Issues

#### The CRM is loading slowly
<Steps>
<Step title="Check your internet connection">
  Ensure you have a stable and fast internet connection.
</Step>

<Step title="Close other browser tabs">
  Close unnecessary browser tabs to free up memory and processing power.
</Step>

<Step title="Clear browser cache">
  Clear your browser's cache and cookies.
</Step>

<Step title="Try a different browser">
  Test the CRM in a different browser to see if the issue is browser-specific.
</Step>

<Step title="Disable browser extensions">
  Some browser extensions can interfere with the CRM's performance.
</Step>
</Steps>

#### Reports are taking too long to load
<Steps>
<Step title="Reduce date range">
  Try narrowing the date range for your report.
</Step>

<Step title="Limit data scope">
  Reduce the scope of data included in the report (e.g., specific teams instead of all teams).
</Step>

<Step title="Use predefined reports">
  Use predefined reports instead of custom reports when possible.
</Step>

<Step title="Schedule reports">
  For complex reports, consider scheduling them to run during off-peak hours.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Integration Issues

### API Connection Problems

#### API requests are returning 401 Unauthorized
<Steps>
<Step title="Verify your API key">
  Double-check that your API key is correct and hasn't expired.
</Step>

<Step title="Check API key permissions">
  Ensure your API key has the necessary permissions for the operations you're trying to perform.
</Step>

<Step title="Verify authorization header">
  Make sure you're including the API key in the Authorization header correctly.
</Step>

<Step title="Check API key status">
  Verify that your API key hasn't been deactivated or suspended.
</Step>
</Steps>

#### Webhooks are not being received
<Steps>
<Step title="Check webhook URL">
  Verify that your webhook URL is accessible from the internet.
</Step>

<Step title="Check SSL certificate">
  If using HTTPS, ensure your SSL certificate is valid.
</Step>

<Step title="Verify webhook configuration">
  Double-check your webhook configuration in the CRM settings.
</Step>

<Step title="Test the endpoint">
  Use a service like webhook.site to test if your endpoint is receiving requests.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Third-Party Integration Issues

#### Email integration not working
<Steps>
<Step title="Verify credentials">
  Check that your email account credentials are correct and current.
</Step>

<Step title="Check security settings">
  Some email providers require app-specific passwords or have security restrictions.
</Step>

<Step title="Review integration settings">
  Ensure the integration is properly configured in both the CRM and the email service.
</Step>

<Step title="Check for rate limits">
  Some email providers have rate limits that might affect integration performance.
</Step>
</Steps>

#### Calendar integration sync problems
<Steps>
<Step title="Re-authorize the connection">
  Try disconnecting and reconnecting the calendar integration.
</Step>

<Step title="Check permissions">
  Ensure the CRM has the necessary permissions to access your calendar.
</Step>

<Step title="Verify calendar settings**:
  Check that your calendar settings allow for the types of events you're trying to sync.
</Step>

<Step title="Check for conflicts**:
  Look for any calendar events that might be causing sync conflicts.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Mobile App Issues

### App Crashes or Freezes

#### App crashes on startup
<Steps>
<Step title="Update the app">
  Ensure you're using the latest version of the mobile app.
</Step>

<Step title="Clear app cache">
  Go to your device's settings and clear the cache for the CRM app.
</Step>

<Step title="Restart your device">
  Sometimes a simple restart can resolve app issues.
</Step>

<Step title="Reinstall the app">
  Uninstall and reinstall the app to ensure a clean installation.
</Step>
</Steps>

#### Data not syncing between mobile and web
<Steps>
<Step title="Check internet connection">
  Ensure your mobile device has a stable internet connection.
</Step>

<Step title="Verify sync settings">
  Check that background app refresh is enabled for the CRM app.
</Step>

<Step title="Check app permissions**:
  Ensure the app has permission to access your network and storage.
</Step>

<Step title="Manual sync**:
  Try manually refreshing the data in the app.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Advanced Troubleshooting

### Data Migration Issues

#### Import is failing
<Steps>
<Step title="Check file format**:
  Ensure your file is in the correct format (CSV, Excel) and follows the required structure.
</Step>

<Step title="Validate data**:
  Check that your data doesn't contain any invalid characters or formatting issues.
</Step>

<Step title="Check file size**:
  Ensure your file doesn't exceed the maximum size limit.
</Step>

<Step title="Review mapping**:
  Verify that your column mappings are correct.
</Step>
</Steps>

#### Duplicate records appearing
<Steps>
<Step title="Check duplicate detection settings**:
  Verify that duplicate detection is enabled and properly configured.
</Step>

<Step title="Review matching criteria**:
  Check the fields used for duplicate detection.
</Step>

<Step title="Manual cleanup**:
  Use the duplicate management tools to merge or remove duplicates.
</Step>

<Step title="Prevention**:
  Implement processes to prevent future duplicates.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Permission and Access Issues

#### Users can't access certain features
<Steps>
<Step title="Check user roles**:
  Verify that the user has the correct role assigned.
</Step>

<Step title="Review role permissions**:
  Check that the role has the necessary permissions for the features in question.
</Step>

<Step title="Check record ownership**:
  Some features may be restricted based on record ownership.
</Step>

<Step title="Verify team settings**:
  Check if there are any team-level restrictions affecting access.
</Step>
</Steps>

#### Data visibility issues
<Steps>
<Step title="Review sharing settings**:
  Check if records need to be shared with specific users or teams.
</Step>

<Step title="Check security settings**:
  Verify that security settings aren't restricting data access.
</Step>

<Step title="Review field-level permissions**:
  Some fields may have restricted access based on user roles.
</Step>

<Step title="Check data ownership**:
  Verify that users have access to the data they need based on ownership rules.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Getting Help

### When to Contact Support

Contact our support team if you've tried the troubleshooting steps above and are still experiencing issues. Provide as much detail as possible about the problem, including:

- What you were trying to do
- What happened instead
- Any error messages you received
- Steps you've already taken to troubleshoot
- Screenshots of the issue if possible
- Browser and device information
- Time and frequency of the issue

### Diagnostic Information

When contacting support, include this diagnostic information:

<Steps>
<Step title="System information">
  Operating system, browser version, and device information.
</Step>

<Step title="Account information**:
  Your account ID and plan type (without sharing sensitive details).
</Step>

<Step title="Error details**:
  Exact error messages and codes received.
</Step>

<Step title="Steps to reproduce**:
  Detailed steps to reproduce the issue.
</Step>

<Step title="Expected vs. actual behavior**:
  What you expected to happen vs. what actually happened.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  **Before contacting support**: Try logging out and back in, clearing your browser cache, or using a different browser to see if the issue persists.
</Tip>

<Note>
  **Maintenance windows**: Scheduled maintenance occurs on Sundays between 2:00 AM and 4:00 AM EST. You'll receive advance notice for any planned downtime.
</Note>

<Warning>
  **Data integrity**: If you suspect a data integrity issue, contact support immediately rather than attempting to fix it yourself.
</Warning>
