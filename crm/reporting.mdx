## Reporting Overview

Lynka CRM includes a comprehensive reporting system with over 40 pre-built reports and customizable dashboards. The reporting engine provides insights into sales performance, lead generation, revenue trends, and team productivity.

## Report Categories

### Executive Reports

#### Revenue Analysis
- **Revenue Over Time**: Monthly revenue trends and patterns
- **Win Rate Trend**: Track your sales team's success rate over time
- **Pipeline Health**: Current pipeline value by stage
- **Net Revenue (Cash Collected)**: Actual cash flow analysis
- **Cost to Acquire Customer (CAC)**: Marketing investment per new customer
- **Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)**: Long-term customer value projections
- **Pipeline Coverage Ratio**: Pipeline value vs. sales targets
- **Sales Cycle Length**: Average time from lead to close

#### Financial Performance
- **Recurring Revenue Growth**: Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) trends
- **Forecast vs Actual**: Weighted pipeline vs. closed revenue comparison
- **Top Customers**: Revenue contribution by customer
- **Revenue Concentration**: Customer concentration analysis

### Sales Performance Reports

#### Team Productivity
- **Sales Leaderboard**: Revenue performance by team member
- **Win Rate by Rep**: Individual sales effectiveness
- **Lead to Opportunity Funnel**: Conversion rates through the pipeline
- **Activity Volume by Rep**: Calls, emails, and meetings per rep
- **Sales Cycle Speed**: Deal velocity by salesperson
- **Quota Attainment**: Progress toward individual targets
- **Discounting Behavior**: Average discounts by salesperson

#### Pipeline Management
- **Stage Conversion Rates**: Movement between pipeline stages
- **Stalled Opportunities**: Deals stuck in stages >14 days
- **Deal Slippage**: Deals pushed to future close dates
- **Lost Reason Analysis**: Why deals are lost
- **Lead Response Time**: Time from lead creation to first contact

### Marketing & Lead Generation Reports

#### Lead Source Analysis
- **Lead Source Mix**: New leads by acquisition channel
- **Revenue by Source**: Revenue attributed to specific channels
- **Lead Quality by Source**: Opportunity conversion by lead source
- **Cost per Lead**: Marketing spend per lead acquired
- **Cost per Opportunity**: Marketing spend per qualified opportunity
- **Campaign Performance**: Lead and revenue by campaign
- **Lead Geography Map**: Geographic distribution of leads

#### Marketing Effectiveness
- **Touch Points to Close**: Average interactions before closing
- **Content Engagement**: Asset engagement driving conversions
- **Unsubscribe/Bounce Rate**: Email marketing health metrics

### Customer Success Reports

#### Retention & Growth
- **Churn Rate**: Customer attrition rates
- **Expansion Revenue**: Upsell/cross-sell revenue from existing customers
- **Customer Satisfaction Score**: NPS and CSAT trends
- **Account Health Score**: Lead quality and engagement metrics

#### Support Analytics
- **Ticket Volume**: Support request trends
- **Time to Resolution**: Average support ticket resolution time
- **Upcoming Renewals**: Contracts expiring in next 90 days

## Creating Custom Reports

### Report Builder

<Steps>
<Step title="Select data source">
  Choose from leads, opportunities, quotes, invoices, or activities.
</Step>

<Step title="Choose metrics">
  Select what you want to measure (counts, sums, averages).
</Step>

<Step title="Add dimensions">
  Choose how to group your data (time, person, category).
</Step>

<Step title="Select visualization">
  Pick the best chart type for your data.
</Step>

<Step title="Apply filters">
  Narrow down to specific time periods or criteria.
</Step>

<Step title="Save and share**:
  Save your report and share with team members.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Available Metrics
- **Count**: Number of records
- **Sum**: Total of numeric values
- **Average**: Mean value of numeric fields
- **Min/Max**: Minimum and maximum values
- **Distinct Count**: Unique values in a field

### Dimension Options
- **Time-based**: Group by day, week, month, quarter, year
- **Entity-based**: Group by salesperson, lead source, product
- **Status-based**: Group by stage, status, or category
- **Geographic**: Group by location or territory

## Performance Optimization

### Report Performance Tips

<Tip>
  **Limit date ranges**: Use appropriate date ranges to avoid processing excessive data.
</Tip>

<Info>
  **Filter early**: Apply filters as early as possible in your report configuration.
</Info>

<Warning>
  **Complex calculations**: Avoid overly complex calculations that can slow down reports.
</Warning>

#### Best Practices
- **Use summary data**: Leverage pre-aggregated data when possible
- **Limit concurrent reports**: Avoid running multiple complex reports simultaneously
- **Schedule off-peak**: Run heavy reports during off-peak hours
- **Optimize filters**: Use specific filters to reduce data processing

### Data Refresh Frequency
- **Real-time reports**: Updated immediately (for simple metrics)
- **Hourly refresh**: Updated every hour (for moderate complexity)
- **Daily refresh**: Updated once daily (for complex aggregations)
- **Manual refresh**: Updated on demand (for ad-hoc analysis)

## Troubleshooting Reports

### Common Issues

#### Slow Report Performance
<Steps>
<Step title="Check date range">
  Ensure date ranges are not unnecessarily broad.
</Step>

<Step title="Review filters">
  Verify filters are properly configured.
</Step>

<Step title="Simplify calculations">
  Reduce complexity of calculated fields.
</Step>

<Step title="Limit data volume">
  Reduce the amount of data being processed.
</Step>
</Steps>

#### Missing Data
- **Check permissions**: Ensure you have access to the required data
- **Verify data sources**: Confirm data exists for the selected period
- **Review filters**: Check if filters are too restrictive
- **Data freshness**: Consider if data is still being processed

#### Incorrect Calculations
- **Verify formulas**: Check calculation logic
- **Review data types**: Ensure correct data types are used
- **Check aggregations**: Confirm proper aggregation methods
- **Validate sources**: Ensure data sources are accurate

<Note>
  **Report caching**: Reports may be cached for performance. Use refresh button to get the latest data when needed.
</Note>
