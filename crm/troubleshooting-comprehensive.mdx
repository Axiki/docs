## Getting Started with Troubleshooting

When encountering issues with Lynka CRM, follow this systematic approach to identify and resolve problems efficiently.

<Steps>
<Step title="Identify the problem**:
  Clearly define what isn't working as expected.
</Step>

<Step title="Check for obvious causes**:
  Verify internet connection, browser, and basic settings.
</Step>

<Step title="Search documentation**:
  Look for solutions in the documentation first.
</Step>

<Step title="Try basic fixes**:
  Clear cache, refresh page, restart browser.
</Step>

<Step title="Test with different user**:
  Determine if the issue is user-specific.
</Step>

<Step title="Contact support**:
  If the issue persists, contact our support team.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Authentication Issues

### Login Problems

#### Cannot Log In
<Steps>
<Step title="Check credentials**:
  Verify your email and password are correct (passwords are case-sensitive).
</Step>

<Step title="Reset password**:
  Use the "Forgot Password" link to reset your password.
</Step>

<Step title="Verify email**:
  Ensure your email address is verified if required.
</Step>

<Step title="Clear browser data**:
  Clear browser cache, cookies, and site data.
</Step>

<Step title="Try different browser**:
  Test with a different browser or incognito window.
</Step>

<Step title="Check for account lockout**:
  Wait if you've made multiple failed attempts.
</Step>
</Steps>

#### Two-Factor Authentication Issues
- **Lost device**: Use backup codes if available
- **Wrong time**: Ensure device time is synchronized
- **App not working**: Try reinstalling the authenticator app
- **No access**: Contact support for account recovery
- **Wrong codes**: Verify the secret key was copied correctly

### Session Problems
- **Frequent logouts**: Check if session timeout is too short
- **Multiple sessions**: Log out of other devices if needed
- **Stuck on loading**: Clear browser cache and cookies
- **Permission errors**: Verify your role and permissions
- **Access denied**: Contact your administrator for access

## Performance Issues

### Slow Loading

#### Page Load Times
<Steps>
<Step title="Check internet connection**:
  Verify your internet speed and stability.
</Step>

<Step title="Close other tabs**:
  Close unnecessary browser tabs and applications.
</Step>

<Step title="Clear browser cache**:
  Clear your browser's cache and cookies.
</Step>

<Step title="Disable extensions**:
  Temporarily disable browser extensions.
</Step>

<Step title="Try different browser**:
  Test performance in another browser.
</Step>

<Step title="Check system resources**:
  Ensure your device has sufficient memory and processing power.
</Step>
</Steps>

#### Database Performance
- **Large data sets**: Use filters to reduce data displayed
- **Complex queries**: Simplify reports and queries
- **Network latency**: Check your connection to the database
- **Peak usage**: Performance may be affected during peak times
- **Browser compatibility**: Ensure you're using a supported browser

### Interface Responsiveness
- **Frozen interface**: Refresh the page or restart the browser
- **Delayed responses**: Check for background processes
- **Unresponsive buttons**: Try clicking again or using keyboard shortcuts
- **Slow typing**: Check for real-time validation or auto-save features
- **Lagging interactions**: Close other applications to free up resources

## Getting Support

### When to Contact Support
- **Persistent issues**: When problems persist after troubleshooting
- **Data loss**: If you experience any data loss
- **Security concerns**: For any security-related issues
- **Integration failures**: When integrations stop working
- **Performance degradation**: For ongoing performance issues

### Information to Provide
- **Detailed description**: Provide detailed description of the issue
- **Steps to reproduce**: Include steps to reproduce the issue
- **Error messages**: Include any error messages received
- **Screenshots**: Provide screenshots if helpful
- **Timeline**: Include when the issue started occurring
- **Environment**: Specify browser, device, and operating system
