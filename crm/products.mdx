## Overview

The product catalog helps you manage your products and services for easy inclusion in quotes and invoices. Each product has pricing, descriptions, and other details that can be reused across multiple documents, ensuring consistency and saving time.

## Adding Products

### New Product Creation

<Steps>
<Step title="Navigate to Products**:
  Go to the Products section in your CRM.
</Step>

<Step title="Click New Product**:
  Select the "New Product" button.
</Step>

<Step title="Enter product details**:
  Add name, description, pricing, and other information.
</Step>

<Step title="Set product attributes**:
  Configure category, SKU, and other attributes.
</Step>

<Step title="Save product**:
  Create the product record in your catalog.
</Step>
</Steps>

#### Required Information
- **Name**: Product or service name (required)
- **Unit Price**: Price per unit (required)
- **Currency**: Currency for the price (required)

#### Optional Information
- **Description**: Detailed product description
- **Category**: Product category for organization
- **SKU**: Stock Keeping Unit identifier
- **Brand**: Product brand information
- **Unit of Measure**: How the product is measured (each, hour, kg, etc.)
- **Barcode**: UPC or other barcode identifier
- **Cost**: Internal cost for margin calculations
- **Tax Rate**: Default tax rate to apply
- **Image URL**: Product image for quotes and invoices
- **Active Status**: Whether the product is currently available

### Bulk Import

Import multiple products at once using CSV files:

<Steps>
<Step title="Prepare your data**:
  Format your product data in CSV with appropriate column headers.
</Step>

<Step title="Navigate to import**:
  Go to Products > Import in your CRM.
</Step>

<Step title="Upload file**:
  Select your CSV file for upload.
</Step>

<Step title="Map fields**:
  Match your CSV columns to product fields.
</Step>

<Step title="Review and confirm**:
  Verify the mapping and start the import.
</Step>
</Steps>

#### CSV Format Requirements
```csv
name,description,unit_price,currency,category,sku,brand,uom,barcode,cost,tax_rate,image_url,active
"Premium Service","Premium consulting service",150,"USD","Services","PRM-CONSULT","Lynka","hour","","120",0.08,"https://example.com/premium-service.jpg",true
"Basic Widget","Basic widget product",25,"USD","Products","BAS-WIDGET","Lynka","each","1234567890123",15,0.0,"https://example.com/basic-widget.jpg",true
```

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### Product Not Appearing in Quotes
<Steps>
<Step title="Check product status**:
  Verify the product is marked as active.
</Step>

<Step title="Verify permissions**:
  Ensure you have permission to access the product.
</Step>

<Step title="Check category filters**:
  Verify category filters aren't hiding the product.
</Step>

<Step title="Search thoroughly**:
  Use different search terms to find the product.
</Step>

<Step title="Contact support**:
  If the issue persists, contact support.
</Step>
</Steps>

#### Pricing Inconsistencies
- **Review product pricing**: Check product pricing settings
- **Verify quote templates**: Ensure templates use correct pricing
- **Check for overrides**: Look for manual price overrides
- **Update as needed**: Make necessary pricing updates
- **Communicate changes**: Inform team members of pricing changes

#### Import Failures
- **Check CSV format**: Verify CSV format and field mapping
- **Validate required fields**: Ensure all required fields are populated
- **Check file size**: Verify file size is within limits
- **Test with sample**: Test with a small sample file first
- **Review error logs**: Check import error logs for details

<Note>
  **Product analytics**: Monitor which products are most frequently used in quotes and invoices to optimize your catalog.
</Note>

<Warning>
  **Price accuracy**: Regularly review and update product prices to ensure accuracy and competitiveness.
</Warning>
