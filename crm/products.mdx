## Overview

Manage your products and services in the product catalog. Each product has pricing, descriptions, and other details that can be reused across quotes and invoices.

## Adding Products

Add products with names, descriptions, pricing, and categories. You can also specify units of measure, brands, and other relevant details. Products can be added to quotes and invoices to streamline the process.

### Required Information
- Name: Product or service name
- Description: Detailed description
- Unit price: Price per unit
- Category: Product category for organization

### Optional Information
- SKU: Stock Keeping Unit identifier
- Brand: Product brand
- Unit of measure: Measurement unit (each, hour, kg, etc.)
- Barcode: UPC or other barcode identifier
- Cost: Internal cost for profit calculations
- Image URL: Product image for quotes and invoices
- Metadata: Additional custom fields
