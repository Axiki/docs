## Welcome to Lynka CRM

Lynka CRM is a comprehensive customer relationship management solution designed for modern businesses. It helps you manage leads, track opportunities, create quotes, process invoices, and collaborate with your team. The platform integrates all the tools you need to manage customer relationships from initial contact to closed deals.

## Key Features

### Lead Management
Track potential customers from initial contact through to conversion with our comprehensive lead management system.

### Opportunity Tracking
Monitor potential deals through your sales pipeline with detailed opportunity tracking.

### Quote & Invoice Creation
Generate professional quotes and convert them to invoices seamlessly.

### Reporting & Analytics
Gain insights into your sales performance with built-in reporting tools.

### Marketing Tools
Built-in tools for lead generation, including WhatsApp links, email signatures, and QR codes.
