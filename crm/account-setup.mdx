## Creating Your Account

Getting started with Lynka CRM is straightforward. You can sign up using your email address or sign in with Google. Once you've created your account, you'll be guided through a quick setup process to configure your basic settings.

<Steps>
<Step title="Sign up for an account">
  Visit the Lynka CRM website and click the "Sign Up" button. You can create an account using your email address or sign in with your Google account.
</Step>

<Step title="Verify your email">
  Check your email inbox for a verification message from Lynka CRM. Click the verification link to confirm your email address.
</Step>

<Step title="Complete the onboarding">
  Follow the onboarding wizard to set up your basic company information, timezone, and preferred currency.
</Step>

<Step title="Configure your profile">
  Add your personal information, contact details, and profile picture to complete your account setup.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Initial Configuration

After creating your account, you'll need to configure some basic settings:

### Company Information
- Company name and address
- Primary contact information
- Business type and industry
- Timezone and currency settings

### User Preferences
- Notification settings
- Theme preferences (light/dark mode)
- Language preferences
- Default dashboard view

## Security Settings

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion icon="shield" title="Password Security">
  Set a strong password with at least 8 characters including uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters. Consider enabling two-factor authentication for additional security.
</Accordion>

<Accordion icon="lock" title="Two-Factor Authentication">
  Enable two-factor authentication to add an extra layer of security to your account. You can use an authenticator app or SMS for verification.
</Accordion>

<Accordion icon="key" title="API Access">
  If you plan to use the API, generate your API keys in the security settings. Keep these keys secure and never share them publicly.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
