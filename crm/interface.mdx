## Dashboard

The dashboard provides an overview of your business metrics and recent activity. It includes widgets showing lead volume, conversion rates, revenue, and other key indicators.

The dashboard is customizable - you can add, remove, and resize widgets to show the metrics that matter most to your business. Widgets include charts, tables, and summary statistics that update in real-time.

## Navigation

The main navigation menu provides access to all sections of the CRM. On mobile devices, the menu collapses to save space while maintaining access to all features.

Navigation items include:
- Dashboard: Business overview and metrics
- LeadGen: Automated lead discovery tools
- Leads: Lead management and tracking
- Opportunities: Deal pipeline management
- Quotes: Proposal and quote management
- Invoices: Billing and payment tracking
- Products: Product catalog management
- Deals: Opportunity management
- Activity: Customer interaction timeline
- Tasks: To-do list and task management
- Reports: Business analytics and insights
- Settings: Account and system configuration

## Data Tables

Data tables display information in organized rows and columns. You can sort, filter, and search through data to find what you need quickly.

Tables include features like column resizing, sorting by clicking headers, and filtering with dropdown menus. You can also select multiple rows for bulk operations like assigning leads or updating statuses.
