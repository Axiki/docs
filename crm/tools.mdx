## Overview

Lynka CRM includes several built-in marketing tools designed to help you generate leads, create professional materials, and automate marketing tasks. These tools are integrated directly into the CRM interface for seamless workflow.

## Lead Generation Tools

### Search Builder (Lead Forge)

The Search Builder is a powerful tool for finding potential customers based on specific criteria:

<Steps>
<Step title="Configure targeting">
  Select your target industry, country, and city.
</Step>

<Step title="Set search parameters">
  Add specific search terms and quality filters.
</Step>

<Step title="Choose run mode">
  Select Normal (5 leads, 1 token) or Turbo (20 leads, 3 tokens).
</Step>

<Step title="Execute search">
  Run the search to discover potential leads.
</Step>

<Step title="Review and accept">
  Review the results and accept leads you want to pursue.
</Step>
</Steps>

#### Search Parameters
- **Industry**: Target specific business sectors
- **Location**: Country and city targeting
- **Search terms**: Keywords for more specific targeting
- **Quality filters**: Minimum rating requirements
- **Run mode**: Normal vs Turbo for volume vs quality

### Presets

Save frequently used search configurations as presets:
- **Quick access**: Instant access to your common searches
- **Color coding**: Visual identification of different presets
- **Easy management**: Rename, update, or delete presets as needed

## Tracking and Analytics

### UTM Builder

Generate UTM parameters for your marketing links to track campaign performance:

<Steps>
<Step title="Enter destination URL">
  Input the URL you want to track.
</Step>

<Step title="Add campaign parameters**:
  Specify campaign name, source, medium, and other parameters.
</Step>

<Step title="Generate tracked link**:
  Create the URL with UTM parameters.
</Step>

<Step title="Use in campaigns**:
  Use the tracked link in your marketing campaigns.
</Step>
</Steps>

#### UTM Parameters
- **Campaign**: Name of your marketing campaign
- **Source**: Source of traffic (google, newsletter, etc.)
- **Medium**: Marketing medium (cpc, email, social, etc.)
- **Term**: Keywords for paid search
- **Content**: Differentiate ads for A/B testing

## Token System

Marketing tools use a token-based system:

- **Lead generation**: Costs 1-3 tokens per search depending on mode
- **Normal mode**: 1 token for up to 5 leads
- **Turbo mode**: 3 tokens for up to 20 leads
- **Free plan**: Limited tokens per month
- **Pro plan**: More tokens per month
- **Referral credits**: Earn tokens by referring others

<Tip>
  **Plan your searches**: Use Normal mode for focused searches and Turbo mode when you need volume.
</Tip>

<Info>
  **Token conservation**: Save tokens by using specific targeting parameters to get better results.
</Info>

<Warning>
  **Token limits**: Monitor your token usage to avoid running out during important campaigns.
</Warning>

## Best Practices

### For Lead Generation
- **Specific targeting**: Use precise industry and location parameters
- **Quality vs. quantity**: Balance between lead volume and quality based on your sales capacity
- **Regular searches**: Run searches regularly to maintain pipeline
- **Preset usage**: Save common search configurations as presets

### For Communication Tools
- **Professional appearance**: Ensure all generated materials look professional
- **Brand consistency**: Use consistent branding across all materials
- **Mobile optimization**: Test all links and materials on mobile devices
- **Performance tracking**: Use UTM parameters to track effectiveness

### For QR Codes
- **High contrast**: Ensure good contrast for easy scanning
- **Adequate size**: Make codes large enough to scan easily
- **Test before printing**: Always test QR codes before mass production
- **Analytics tracking**: Use analytics to track QR code effectiveness

## Integration with CRM

### Lead Flow
1. **Discovery**: Leads found through Search Builder
2. **Review**: Evaluate and accept leads in the CRM
3. **Enrichment**: Additional data gathered about accepted leads
4. **CRM integration**: Leads appear in your main leads list
5. **Follow-up**: Engage with leads using CRM tools

### Data Enrichment
Marketing tools can automatically enrich lead data:
- **Website verification**: Confirm website existence
- **Email validation**: Verify email addresses
- **Social profile links**: Connect to social media profiles
- **Company information**: Gather company details and industry

<Note>
  **Token management**: Some marketing tools consume tokens from your monthly allocation. Monitor usage to optimize your marketing efforts.
</Note>
