## Account Setup

### Creating Your Account

<Steps>
<Step title="Sign up**:
  Visit the Lynka CRM website and click "Sign Up". You can sign up with your email address or use Google authentication.
</Step>

<Step title="Verify your email**:
  Check your email inbox for a verification message and click the verification link.
</Step>

<Step title="Complete profile**:
  Add your name, company information, and contact details.
</Step>

<Step title="Choose your plan**:
  Select the plan that best fits your business needs.
</Step>

<Step title="Set up security**:
  Configure two-factor authentication for enhanced security.
</Step>

<Step title="Complete onboarding**:
  Follow the onboarding wizard to configure basic settings.
</Step>
</Steps>

#### Required Information
- **Email address**: Your primary email for account access
- **Company name**: Your business name
- **Business type**: Industry and business model
- **Timezone**: Your local timezone for scheduling
- **Currency**: Base currency for all financial data
- **Phone number**: For two-factor authentication

## Understanding the Interface

### Main Navigation

The Lynka CRM interface is organized around your sales process:

- **Dashboard**: Overview of key metrics and recent activity
- **LeadGen**: Automated lead discovery tools
- **Leads**: Manage potential customers
- **Opportunities**: Track potential deals
- **Quotes**: Create and manage proposals
- **Invoices**: Generate and track billing
- **Products**: Manage your product catalog
- **Reports**: Analyze your business performance
- **Settings**: Configure your account and preferences

### Dashboard Overview

Your dashboard provides a snapshot of your business performance:
- **Key metrics**: Revenue, leads, conversions, and other KPIs
- **Recent activity**: Latest customer interactions
- **Upcoming tasks**: Important deadlines and follow-ups
- **Performance trends**: Historical performance data

<Info>
  **Customizable**: Your dashboard is fully customizable to show the metrics that matter most to your business.
</Info>

## Core CRM Modules

### Lead Management

#### Adding Your First Lead

<Steps>
<Step title="Navigate to Leads**:
  Click on "Leads" in the main navigation.
</Step>

<Step title="Create new lead**:
  Click the "New Lead" button.
</Step>

<Step title="Enter lead information**:
  Add the contact's name, company, email, and phone number.
</Step>

<Step title="Set lead status**:
  Select an appropriate status (New, Contacted, Qualified, etc.).
</Step>

<Step title="Add notes**:
  Include any relevant information about the lead.
</Step>

<Step title="Save lead**:
  Click "Save" to add the lead to your database.
</Step>
</Steps>

#### Lead Statuses Explained
- **New**: Recently added lead that hasn't been contacted
- **Contacted**: Lead has been reached out to
- **Qualified**: Lead shows genuine interest and fits your criteria
- **Proposal**: Lead has received a quote or proposal
- **Won**: Lead converted to a customer
- **Lost**: Lead didn't convert

### Opportunity Management

#### Creating Opportunities

When a lead shows strong interest, convert them to an opportunity:

<Steps>
<Step title="Select qualified lead**:
  Choose a lead that has shown genuine interest.
</Step>

<Step title="Convert to opportunity**:
  Click "Convert to Opportunity" from the lead actions.
</Step>

<Step title="Enter opportunity details**:
  Add expected value, probability, and close date.
</Step>

<Step title="Assign to owner**:
  Select the salesperson responsible for the opportunity.
</Step>

<Step title="Set stage**:
  Place the opportunity in the appropriate pipeline stage.
</Step>

<Step title="Save opportunity**:
  Create the opportunity record linked to the original lead.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Quote Creation

#### Building Your First Quote

<Steps>
<Step title="Navigate to Quotes**:
  Go to the Quotes section in your CRM.
</Step>

<Step title="Create new quote**:
  Click "New Quote" to start building.
</Step>

<Step title="Select associated lead**:
  Choose the lead this quote is for.
</Step>

<Step title="Add line items**:
  Include products/services with descriptions and pricing.
</Step>

<Step title="Apply discounts**:
  Add any applicable discounts or adjustments.
</Step>

<Step title="Send to client**:
  Send the quote to your client for review.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Marketing Tools

### Lead Generation

#### Using Lead Forge

The Lead Forge helps you discover new prospects automatically:

<Steps>
<Step title="Configure search parameters**:
  Set your target industry, location, and other criteria.
</Step>

<Step title="Choose run mode**:
  Select Normal (5 leads, 1 token) or Turbo (20 leads, 3 tokens).
</Step>

<Step title="Execute search**:
  Run the search to discover potential leads.
</Step>

<Step title="Review results**:
  Examine the leads returned by the system.
</Step>

<Step title="Accept relevant leads**:
  Accept leads you want to pursue.
</Step>

<Step title="Add to CRM**:
  Import accepted leads into your main database.
</Step>
</Steps>

#### Marketing Automation Tools
- **WhatsApp Link Generator**: Create custom WhatsApp links
- **Email Signature Generator**: Build professional email signatures
- **Business Card QR Generator**: Create QR codes with contact info
- **UTM Builder**: Generate tracking parameters for marketing links

## Best Practices for Success

### Data Quality

#### Maintaining Clean Data
<Warning>
  **Data hygiene**: Clean data is essential for accurate reporting and effective CRM usage.
</Warning>

- **Complete records**: Fill in all available information for each record
- **Consistent formatting**: Use consistent formats for phone numbers, addresses, etc.
- **Regular cleanup**: Remove duplicates and outdated information regularly
- **Validation**: Use validation rules to maintain data quality
- **Standardization**: Establish standards for data entry across your team

### Regular Maintenance

#### Weekly Tasks
- **Review leads**: Check for leads that need follow-up
- **Update opportunities**: Update opportunity status and next steps
- **Clean data**: Remove duplicates and update outdated information
- **Review reports**: Analyze weekly performance metrics
- **Plan follow-ups**: Schedule follow-up activities for the week

#### Monthly Tasks
- **Performance review**: Analyze monthly performance against goals
- **Pipeline review**: Review sales pipeline health and conversion rates
- **Team performance**: Review individual team member performance
- **Process optimization**: Identify and improve inefficient processes
- **Training**: Provide additional training on CRM features
