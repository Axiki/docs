## Overview

Opportunities represent potential deals that are actively being pursued. Each opportunity is linked to a lead and tracks the potential revenue and probability of closing. The opportunity management system helps you forecast revenue and manage your sales pipeline effectively.

## Creating Opportunities

### From Leads

Convert qualified leads to opportunities:

<Steps>
<Step title="Select qualified lead**:
  Choose a lead that shows genuine interest and fits your criteria.
</Step>

<Step title="Click convert to opportunity**:
  Select "Convert to Opportunity" from the lead actions.
</Step>

<Step title="Enter opportunity details**:
  Add expected value, probability, and close date.
</Step>

<Step title="Assign to owner**:
  Select the salesperson responsible for the opportunity.
</Step>

<Step title="Save opportunity**:
  Create the opportunity record linked to the original lead.
</Step>
</Steps>

#### Conversion Benefits
<Tip>
  **Data preservation**: All lead information transfers to the opportunity, maintaining complete history and context.
</Tip>

- **Complete history**: All lead interactions and notes transfer
- **Contact information**: Lead contact details automatically populate
- **Company details**: Company information carries over
- **Source tracking**: Lead source information preserved
- **Custom fields**: Any custom lead fields transfer appropriately

### Direct Creation

Create opportunities without converting from a lead:

<Steps>
<Step title="Navigate to Opportunities**:
  Go to the Opportunities section in your CRM.
</Step>

<Step title="Click New Opportunity**:
  Select the "New Opportunity" button.
</Step>

<Step title="Enter opportunity details**:
  Add title, value, probability, and other information.
</Step>

<Step title="Link to lead**:
  Optionally connect to an existing lead.
</Step>

<Step title="Save opportunity**:
  Create the new opportunity record.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Opportunity Information

### Required Fields
- **Title**: Descriptive name for the opportunity (required)
- **Expected Value**: Potential revenue from the deal (required)
- **Currency**: Currency for all monetary values (required)
- **Probability**: Likelihood of closing the deal (required)

### Optional Fields
- **Lead**: Link to the associated lead
- **Owner**: Salesperson responsible for the opportunity
- **Stage**: Current stage in the sales process
- **Expected Close Date**: Estimated date for closing the deal
- **Notes**: Additional information about the opportunity
- **Tags**: Categorization labels
- **Source**: How the opportunity was generated
- **Competitors**: Competitors involved in the deal
- **Decision Criteria**: Factors that will influence the decision
- **Decision Process**: Steps the customer will take to make a decision

## Opportunity Stages

### Stage Definitions

#### New
Initial opportunity created. The potential deal has been identified but not yet qualified. This is the earliest stage in the sales process.

#### Qualified
Opportunity validated with clear requirements, budget, and decision maker identified. The customer has confirmed they have a need and budget for your solution.

#### Proposition
Formal proposal or quote has been submitted to the prospect. This stage indicates you've presented your solution and are waiting for a response.

#### Won
Deal closed successfully. The opportunity has converted to a customer and revenue has been recognized.

#### Lost
Opportunity didn't materialize. The customer chose a competitor, decided not to buy, or the deal fell through for other reasons.

#### Stage Probabilities
- **New**: 10% probability of closing
- **Qualified**: 25% probability of closing
- **Proposition**: 50% probability of closing
- **Won**: 100% probability of closing
- **Lost**: 0% probability of closing

## Opportunity Values

### Expected Amount
The potential revenue value of the opportunity. This contributes to your pipeline value and forecasting. The expected amount should reflect the realistic potential value of the deal.

### Probability
The likelihood of closing the deal, expressed as a percentage. Multiply this by the expected amount to get the weighted value. Probability should be based on evidence and experience rather than optimism.

### Weighted Value
Calculated as Expected Amount × Probability. This represents the realistic expected revenue from the opportunity and is used for forecasting.

### Currency
Specify the currency for the opportunity value to ensure accurate reporting and forecasting. The system supports multiple currencies and can handle conversion for reporting purposes.

## Opportunity Management Features

### Pipeline Tracking
Monitor opportunities through your sales pipeline with stage-based tracking and conversion metrics. The pipeline view shows all opportunities organized by their current stage.

### Forecasting
Use opportunity values and probabilities to forecast future revenue. The forecasting system calculates weighted pipeline value and projected close dates.

### Assignment
Assign opportunities to specific team members based on expertise, territory, or other criteria. Assignment ensures accountability and proper follow-up.

### Tags
Use tags to categorize and organize opportunities for easier searching and filtering. Tags can represent industries, deal types, or other business-specific categories.

### Notes
Add notes to track interactions and important information about each opportunity. Notes help maintain context and share information with team members.

### History
View the complete history of stage changes and interactions with each opportunity. The history includes who made changes and when they occurred.

## Opportunity Analytics

### Key Metrics

#### Conversion Rates
Track conversion rates between stages to identify pipeline bottlenecks:
- **New to Qualified**: Percentage of new opportunities that become qualified
- **Qualified to Proposition**: Percentage of qualified opportunities that receive proposals
- **Proposition to Won**: Percentage of proposed opportunities that close successfully

#### Velocity Metrics
- **Average cycle time**: Average time from opportunity creation to close
- **Stage duration**: Average time spent in each stage
- **Movement rate**: How quickly opportunities move through stages

#### Value Metrics
- **Pipeline value**: Total value of all active opportunities
- **Weighted pipeline**: Pipeline value adjusted for probability
- **Forecast accuracy**: How accurate your forecasts are compared to actual results

### Performance Tracking

#### Individual Performance
- **Opportunity count**: Number of opportunities managed by each rep
- **Win rate**: Percentage of opportunities won by each rep
- **Average deal size**: Average value of won opportunities by rep
- **Pipeline velocity**: Speed of opportunity movement by rep

#### Team Performance
- **Team pipeline**: Total pipeline value by team
- **Team win rate**: Overall team win rate
- **Team conversion**: Team performance by stage
- **Team forecasting**: Team forecasting accuracy

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### Stalled Opportunities
<Steps>
<Step title="Review opportunity**:
  Examine opportunities that haven't moved in 30+ days.
</Step>

<Step title="Contact customer**:
  Reach out to understand the status of the deal.
</Step>

<Step title="Update information**:
  Update opportunity details based on new information.
</Step>

<Step title="Adjust probability**:
  Modify probability based on current status.
</Step>

<Step title="Set follow-up**:
  Schedule follow-up activities if needed.
</Step>
</Steps>

#### Inaccurate Forecasting
- **Review probabilities**: Check if probability estimates are realistic
- **Update values**: Verify opportunity values are current
- **Stage accuracy**: Ensure stage information is accurate
- **Historical analysis**: Compare forecasts to actual results
- **Adjust methodology**: Modify forecasting approach based on accuracy

#### Poor Stage Conversion
- **Analyze reasons**: Identify why opportunities aren't moving between stages
- **Qualification review**: Review qualification criteria and processes
- **Sales process**: Optimize your sales process based on conversion data
- **Training**: Provide additional training on sales process
- **Tools**: Ensure team has necessary tools and resources

<Note>
  **Opportunity nurturing**: Some opportunities take time to mature. Implement follow-up processes for longer sales cycles.
</Note>

<Warning>
  **Probability accuracy**: Be realistic with probability estimates to ensure accurate forecasting.
</Warning>
