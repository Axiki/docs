## Overview

Opportunities represent potential deals that are actively being pursued. Each opportunity is linked to a lead and tracks the potential revenue and probability of closing.

## Creating Opportunities

When a lead shows strong interest, you can convert them to an opportunity. The system will automatically create an opportunity record and maintain the connection to the original lead. Opportunities have stages that mirror the sales process.

You can also create opportunities directly without converting from a lead. This is useful for:
- Existing customer upsells
- Warm prospects from other sources
- Internal sales initiatives

## Opportunity Stages

Opportunities progress through the following stages:

- New: Initial opportunity created
- Qualified: Opportunity validated with clear requirements
- Proposition: Formal proposal submitted
- Won: Deal closed successfully
- Lost: Opportunity didn't materialize

## Opportunity Details

Each opportunity includes important information:

- Title: Descriptive name for the opportunity
- Expected amount: Potential revenue from the deal
- Currency: Currency for financial values
- Probability: Likelihood of closing the deal (percentage)
- Owner: Team member responsible for the opportunity
- Expected close date: Estimated date for closing the deal

## Forecasting

Use opportunity data for sales forecasting:
- Calculate weighted pipeline value
- Track conversion rates by stage
- Identify bottlenecks in your sales process
- Predict future revenue based on current opportunities
