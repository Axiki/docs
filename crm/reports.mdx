## Built-in Reports

Access pre-built reports covering sales performance, lead conversion, revenue trends, and other key metrics. These reports provide insights into your business performance.

Built-in reports include lead sources, conversion rates, revenue by period, and sales team performance. Each report can be filtered by date range, team member, or other criteria.

## Custom Reports

Create custom reports tailored to your specific business needs. You can select which data to include and how to visualize it.

The report builder allows you to select data sources, apply filters, and choose visualization types. You can create charts, tables, and other visualizations to highlight important trends and patterns.

## Report Scheduling

Schedule reports to run automatically and be delivered to your email or shared with team members. This ensures you always have the latest data without manual effort.

Set up scheduled reports by configuring the frequency, recipients, and delivery method. Reports can be sent daily, weekly, monthly, or on custom schedules. You can also configure different reports for different team members.
