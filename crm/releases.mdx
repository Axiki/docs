## Release Schedule

We typically release updates on a bi-weekly basis. Major feature releases occur monthly, with minor updates and bug fixes released as needed.

### Release Process
1. **Development**: Features are developed in isolated branches
2. **Testing**: Comprehensive testing in staging environment
3. **Review**: Quality assurance and user acceptance testing
4. **Deployment**: Gradual rollout to production servers
5. **Monitoring**: Post-release monitoring and support

<Tip>
  **Stay Updated**: Enable notifications in your account settings to receive alerts about new releases and important updates.
</Tip>

<Note>
  **Maintenance Windows**: Scheduled maintenance occurs on Sundays between 2:00 AM and 4:00 AM EST. You'll receive advance notice for any planned downtime.
</Note>
