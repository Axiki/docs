## WhatsApp Link Generator

Create customized WhatsApp links that allow customers to contact you directly. The tool generates links with pre-filled messages, making it easy for prospects to reach out.

Simply enter your phone number and optional message text. The tool will generate a clickable link that opens WhatsApp with your number and message pre-filled. This is great for marketing materials and social media profiles.

## Email Signature Generator

Build professional email signatures that include your contact information, company details, and social media links. The generator creates HTML signatures that work across email platforms.

Enter your name, title, company, phone number, and other details. You can also add your company logo and social media links. The tool generates a signature that maintains consistent branding across all your emails.

## Business Card QR Generator

Create QR codes that contain your contact information. When scanned, these codes automatically add your contact details to the user's phone.

Input your name, company, phone, email, website, and other contact details. The generator creates a QR code that can be printed on business cards, brochures, or other materials. Scanning the code instantly adds your contact information to the user's contacts.

## UTM Builder

Generate UTM parameters for your marketing links to track campaign performance. This helps you understand which marketing efforts are most effective.

Enter the URL you want to track, campaign name, source, medium, and other parameters. The tool generates a full URL with UTM parameters that can be tracked in your analytics platform.
