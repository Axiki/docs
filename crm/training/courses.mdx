## Course Structure

Each course is designed to be self-paced and includes:

- **Video lessons**: Engaging video content with real-world examples
- **Hands-on exercises**: Practical exercises using the CRM interface
- **Quizzes**: Knowledge checks to reinforce learning
- **Projects**: Real-world projects to apply your skills
- **Certificates**: Completion certificates for your professional portfolio

## Course Details

### CRM Fundamentals
- **Duration**: 2 hours
- **Level**: Beginner
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Topics covered**:
  - Introduction to CRM concepts
  - Navigating the interface
  - Adding and managing contacts
  - Basic reporting
  - Getting help and support

### Lead Management Essentials
- **Duration**: 3 hours
- **Level**: Beginner
- **Prerequisites**: CRM Fundamentals or equivalent experience
- **Topics covered**:
  - Lead capture strategies
  - Lead qualification techniques
  - Lead nurturing workflows
  - Conversion optimization
  - Lead scoring methods

### Sales Pipeline Optimization
- **Duration**: 4 hours
- **Level**: Intermediate
- **Prerequisites**: Lead Management Essentials
- **Topics covered**:
  - Pipeline stages and definitions
  - Conversion rate optimization
  - Forecasting techniques
  - Bottleneck identification
  - Performance metrics

### Advanced Reporting
- **Duration**: 5 hours
- **Level**: Intermediate
- **Prerequisites**: Sales Pipeline Optimization
- **Topics covered**:
  - Custom report creation
  - Data visualization techniques
  - Dashboard design
  - Automated reporting
  - Data analysis methods

### CRM Automation
- **Duration**: 6 hours
- **Level**: Advanced
- **Prerequisites**: Advanced Reporting
- **Topics covered**:
  - Workflow automation
  - Trigger configuration
  - Conditional logic
  - Integration automation
  - Performance monitoring

### Team Collaboration
- **Duration**: 4 hours
- **Level**: Advanced
- **Prerequisites**: CRM Automation
- **Topics covered**:
  - Role-based permissions
  - Team workflows
  - Communication tools
  - Performance tracking
  - Best practices
