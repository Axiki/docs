## About Certification

The Lynka CRM Certification Program validates your expertise in using our customer relationship management platform. Certification demonstrates your proficiency to employers and clients, enhancing your professional credibility.

## Certification Levels

### Associate Certification
- **Target audience**: New users and beginners
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Exam format**: Multiple choice and scenario-based questions
- **Duration**: 90 minutes
- **Passing score**: 70% or higher
- **Valid for**: 2 years

#### Topics Covered:
- CRM fundamentals and navigation
- Lead management basics
- Contact and account management
- Basic reporting
- User interface and settings

### Professional Certification
- **Target audience**: Experienced users and administrators
- **Prerequisites**: Associate certification or equivalent experience
- **Exam format**: Multiple choice, scenario-based, and hands-on exercises
- **Duration**: 3 hours
- **Passing score**: 75% or higher
- **Valid for**: 2 years

#### Topics Covered:
- Advanced lead management
- Opportunity and pipeline management
- Quote and invoice creation
- Advanced reporting and analytics
- Team management and permissions
- Integration basics

### Expert Certification
- **Target audience**: Advanced users, consultants, and administrators
- **Prerequisites**: Professional certification or equivalent experience
- **Exam format**: Comprehensive exam with practical exercises
- **Duration**: 4 hours
- **Passing score**: 80% or higher
- **Valid for**: 2 years

#### Topics Covered:
- Advanced automation and workflows
- Customization and configuration
- Advanced integrations
- Performance optimization
- Security and compliance
- Troubleshooting and support

## Maintaining Certification

### Continuing Education
To maintain your certification, you must complete continuing education requirements:

- **Associate**: 10 hours every 2 years
- **Professional**: 20 hours every 2 years
- **Expert**: 30 hours every 2 years

### Recertification
Certifications are valid for 2 years. Before expiration, you can:
- Complete continuing education requirements
- Pass a recertification exam
- Attend approved training events
