## Common Issues

### Login Problems

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion icon="lock" title="I can't log in to my account">
  <Steps>
    <Step title="Check your credentials">
      Make sure you're using the correct email and password. Passwords are case-sensitive.
    </Step>
    
    <Step title="Reset your password">
      If you've forgotten your password, use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page to reset it.
    </Step>
    
    <Step title="Check your email verification">
      If you just created an account, make sure you've verified your email address by clicking the link in the verification email.
    </Step>
    
    <Step title="Clear your browser cache">
      Sometimes clearing your browser's cache and cookies can resolve login issues.
    </Step>
  </Steps>
</Accordion>

<Accordion icon="wifi" title="I'm experiencing slow loading times">
  <ul>
    <li>Check your internet connection speed</li>
    <li>Try using a different browser</li>
    <li>Disable browser extensions that might interfere</li>
    <li>Clear your browser cache and cookies</li>
    <li>Try accessing the CRM from a different network</li>
  </ul>
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

### Data Issues

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion icon="database" title="Data is not saving or updating">
  <Steps>
    <Step title="Check your internet connection">
      Ensure you have a stable internet connection. The CRM requires an active connection to save data.
    </Step>
    
    <Step title="Refresh the page">
      Try refreshing the page to see if the data appears. Sometimes there's a delay in synchronization.
    </Step>
    
    <Step title="Check for error messages">
      Look for any error messages that might indicate what went wrong during the save process.
    </Step>
    
    <Step title="Try again">
      Attempt to save the data again after a few seconds.
    </Step>
  </Steps>
</Accordion>

<Accordion icon="search" title="I can't find a record I know exists">
  <ul>
    <li>Check your search filters - you might have filters applied that are hiding the record</li>
    <li>Try searching with different keywords</li>
    <li>Check if the record might be in the trash/recycle bin</li>
    <li>Verify the record wasn't accidentally deleted</li>
    <li>Check if you have the necessary permissions to view the record</li>
  </ul>
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

### Email and Notifications

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion icon="envelope" title="Email notifications are not arriving">
  <Steps>
    <Step title="Check your spam folder">
      Sometimes email notifications end up in your spam or junk folder.
    </Step>
    
    <Step title="Verify your email settings">
      Go to your profile settings and confirm your email address is correct.
    </Step>
    
    <Step title="Check notification preferences">
      Make sure you have email notifications enabled in your account settings.
    </Step>
    
    <Step title="Whitelist our domain">
      Add our email domain to your email provider's whitelist to prevent filtering.
    </Step>
  </Steps>
</Accordion>

<Accordion icon="bell" title="I'm receiving too many notifications">
  <ul>
    <li>Adjust your notification preferences in Settings > Notifications</li>
    <li>Turn off specific notification types you don't need</li>
    <li>Set up notification schedules to receive updates only during certain times</li>
    <li>Unsubscribe from specific notification categories</li>
  </ul>
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

### Mobile App Issues

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion icon="mobile" title="Mobile app is crashing or freezing">
  <Steps>
    <Step title="Update the app">
      Make sure you're using the latest version of the mobile app.
    </Step>
    
    <Step title="Clear app cache">
      Go to your device's settings and clear the cache for the CRM app.
    </Step>
    
    <Step title="Restart your device">
      Sometimes a simple restart can resolve app issues.
    </Step>
    
    <Step title="Reinstall the app">
      Uninstall and reinstall the app to ensure a clean installation.
    </Step>
  </Steps>
</Accordion>

<Accordion icon="sync" title="Mobile app data is not syncing">
  <ul>
    <li>Check your internet connection on your mobile device</li>
    <li>Ensure background app refresh is enabled for the CRM app</li>
    <li>Check if the app has permission to access your network</li>
    <li>Try manually refreshing the data in the app</li>
    <li>Sign out and sign back in to refresh the connection</li>
  </ul>
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Performance Issues

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion icon="bolt" title="CRM feels slow or unresponsive">
  <Steps>
    <Step title="Check your browser">
      Use the latest version of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge for best performance.
    </Step>
    
    <Step title="Close other tabs">
      Close unnecessary browser tabs to free up memory and processing power.
    </Step>
    
    <Step title="Check your internet speed">
      Ensure you have sufficient upload and download speeds for the CRM to function properly.
    </Step>
    
    <Step title="Disable extensions">
      Disable browser extensions that might interfere with the CRM's performance.
    </Step>
  </Steps>
</Accordion>

<Accordion icon="memory" title="Browser is consuming too much memory">
  <ul>
    <li>Close other browser tabs and windows</li>
    <li>Clear your browser's cache and cookies</li>
    <li>Restart your browser periodically</li>
    <li>Consider using a different browser</li>
    <li>Check if your device has sufficient RAM</li>
  </ul>
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Integration Problems

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion icon="plug" title="Third-party integration is not working">
  <Steps>
    <Step title="Check integration settings">
      Verify that the integration is properly configured in Settings > Integrations.
    </Step>
    
    <Step title="Re-authorize the connection">
      Try disconnecting and reconnecting the integration to refresh the authorization.
    </Step>
    
    <Step title="Check API limits">
      Verify that you haven't exceeded any API rate limits imposed by the third-party service.
    </Step>
    
    <Step title="Review error logs">
      Check the integration logs for specific error messages that might indicate the issue.
    </Step>
  </Steps>
</Accordion>

<Accordion icon="code" title="API requests are failing">
  <ul>
    <li>Verify your API key is correct and hasn't expired</li>
    <li>Check if you're exceeding rate limits</li>
    <li>Ensure your requests are properly formatted</li>
    <li>Check the API documentation for any recent changes</li>
    <li>Verify your IP address is not blocked</li>
  </ul>
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Getting Help

<Note>
  If you've tried the troubleshooting steps above and are still experiencing issues, please contact our support team. Provide as much detail as possible about the problem, including:
</Note>

<ul>
  <li>What you were trying to do</li>
  <li>What happened instead</li>
  <li>Any error messages you received</li>
  <li>Steps you've already taken to troubleshoot</li>
  <li>Screenshots of the issue if possible</li>
  <li>Browser and device information</li>
</ul>

<Tip>
  **Pro tip**: Before contacting support, try logging out and back in, clearing your browser cache, or using a different browser to see if the issue persists.
</Tip>
