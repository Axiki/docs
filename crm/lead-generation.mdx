## Overview

The Lynka CRM Lead Generation system (called "Lead Forge") is a powerful tool that helps you discover and capture new prospects automatically. The system uses advanced search algorithms to find potential customers based on your specified criteria.

## Search Builder

The Search Builder is the core tool for finding leads based on your criteria:

<Steps>
<Step title="Define your search criteria">
  Specify industries, locations, and other parameters.
</Step>

<Step title="Configure search settings">
  Set quality thresholds, result limits, and filtering options.
</Step>

<Step title="Run the search">
  Execute your search to discover potential leads.
</Step>

<Step title="Review results">
  Examine the leads returned by the system.
</Step>

<Step title="Accept or discard">
  Accept leads you want to pursue or discard irrelevant ones.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Search Modes

#### Normal Mode
- Up to 5 leads per search
- Uses 1 token per search
- Standard quality filters
- Good for focused searches

#### Turbo Mode
- Up to 20 leads per search
- Uses 3 tokens per search
- More relaxed quality filters
- Better for volume searches

### Quality Filters

The system applies several quality filters to ensure lead quality:

- **Website requirement**: Leads must have a verified website
- **Email requirement**: Leads must have a verified email address
- **Rating threshold**: Minimum Google Maps rating (adjustable)
- **Duplicate detection**: Automatic detection of existing leads

## Lead Results Management

### Review Process

After running a search, you'll see candidate leads that you can:

- **Accept**: Add the lead to your main CRM database
- **Discard**: Remove the lead from consideration
- **Hide**: Temporarily hide the lead from view
- **Tag**: Add tags for organization and filtering

### Lead Information

Each lead includes:

- **Name**: Contact person name
- **Company**: Company name
- **Email**: Contact email address
- **Phone**: Phone number
- **Website**: Company website
- **Address**: Physical address
- **City**: Location information
- **Rating**: Google Maps rating (if available)
- **Category**: Business category
- **Source Query**: How the lead was discovered

## Token System

Lead generation uses a token-based system:

- **Normal searches**: 1 token per search
- **Turbo searches**: 3 tokens per search
- **Free plan**: Limited tokens per month
- **Pro plan**: More tokens per month
- **Referral credits**: Additional tokens earned through referrals

<Tip>
  **Start conservative**: Begin with Normal mode to understand the quality of results before using Turbo mode.
</Tip>

<Info>
  **Geographic focus**: Narrow your geographic search area for better results in specific markets.
</Info>

<Warning>
  **Token management**: Monitor your token usage to avoid running out during important searches.
</Warning>

## Best Practices

### Search Optimization
- **Specific industries**: Use precise industry terms for better targeting
- **Location precision**: Target specific cities rather than broad regions
- **Quality balance**: Adjust search mode based on your sales capacity
- **Regular searches**: Run searches regularly to maintain pipeline

### Lead Follow-up
- **Prompt contact**: Reach out to new leads quickly for better conversion
- **Personalization**: Use the discovered information to personalize outreach
- **Tracking**: Monitor which search parameters yield the best leads
- **Refinement**: Adjust search settings based on lead quality results

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### No Results Found
- **Check filters**: Ensure your quality filters aren't too restrictive
- **Expand location**: Try a broader geographic area
- **Adjust industry**: Try related industry terms
- **Try different keywords**: Experiment with search terms

#### Low Quality Results
- **Increase rating threshold**: Raise the minimum rating requirement
- **Use Normal mode**: Switch from Turbo to Normal mode for higher quality
- **Narrow search**: Focus on more specific industries or locations
- **Add search terms**: Include more specific keywords

<Note>
  **Rate limits**: The system has built-in rate limits to ensure fair usage and prevent API abuse.
</Note>
