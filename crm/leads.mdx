## Overview

The lead management system is the foundation of your CRM, helping you track potential customers from initial contact through to conversion. Each lead contains contact information, company details, and interaction history that enables effective sales processes.

## Adding Leads

### Manual Lead Creation

<Steps>
<Step title="Navigate to Leads**:
  Go to the Leads section in your CRM.
</Step>

<Step title="Click New Lead**:
  Select the "New Lead" button.
</Step>

<Step title="Enter lead information**:
  Add contact details, company information, and other relevant data.
</Step>

<Step title="Set lead status**:
  Assign an appropriate status based on the lead's current state.
</Step>

<Step title="Save lead**:
  Create the lead record in your database.
</Step>
</Steps>

#### Required Fields
- **Name**: Contact person's name (required)
- **Company**: Company name (required)
- **Email**: Contact email address (required)

#### Optional Fields
- **Phone**: Phone number
- **Mobile Phone**: Mobile phone number
- **Website**: Company website URL
- **Address**: Physical address
- **City**: Location information
- **Job Title**: Contact's position
- **Industry**: Company industry
- **Notes**: Additional information
- **Tags**: Categorization labels
- **Source**: How the lead was acquired
- **Priority**: Lead priority level
- **Rating**: Lead rating (1-5 scale)

### Bulk Import

## Lead Statuses

### Status Definitions

<Tip>
  **Consistent status updates**: Regularly update lead statuses to maintain accurate pipeline reporting.
</Tip>

#### New
Recently added leads that haven't been contacted yet. These are at the top of your prospecting list and require immediate attention.

#### Contacted
Leads that have been reached out to. This indicates initial contact has been made and the lead is being actively pursued.

#### Qualified
Leads that show genuine interest and fit your target criteria. These leads have budget, authority, need, and timeline (BANT).

#### Proposal
Leads that have received a quote or proposal. They are actively considering your offering and are closer to conversion.

#### Won
Converted leads that became customers. These move to your customer database and are no longer tracked as leads.

#### Lost
Leads that didn't convert. Document the reason for future reference and potential re-engagement strategies.

#### Manual Status Updates
- **Individual updates**: Update single lead statuses manually

## Lead Conversion

### Converting to Opportunities

When a lead shows strong interest, convert them to an opportunity:

<Steps>
<Step title="Select qualified lead**:
  Choose a lead that has shown genuine interest.
</Step>

<Step title="Click convert**:
  Select "Convert to Opportunity" from the lead actions.
</Step>

<Step title="Enter opportunity details**:
  Add value, probability, and other opportunity-specific information.
</Step>

<Step title="Save opportunity**:
  Create the opportunity record linked to the original lead.
</Step>
</Steps>

#### Conversion Benefits
- **Data preservation**: All lead data transfers to opportunity
- **History maintenance**: Complete interaction history preserved
- **Pipeline tracking**: Opportunity enters sales pipeline
- **Revenue tracking**: Potential revenue becomes trackable
- **Forecasting**: Opportunity contributes to sales forecasting

### Lead Qualification Frameworks

#### BANT Framework
- **Budget**: Does the lead have budget for your solution?
- **Authority**: Does the lead have authority to make purchasing decisions?
- **Need**: Does the lead have a genuine need for your solution?
- **Timeline**: What is the lead's timeline for making a decision?

#### CHAMP Framework
- **Challenges**: What challenges does the lead face?
- **Authority**: Who has decision-making authority?
- **Money**: Is there budget allocated for the solution?
- **Prioritization**: How does this rank in their priorities?
