## Overview

The lead management system helps you track potential customers from initial contact through to conversion. You can add new leads, update their status, and track their progress through your sales pipeline.

## Adding New Leads

To add a new lead, go to the Leads section and click the "New Lead" button. Fill in the contact information, company details, and any relevant notes. The system will automatically assign a status of "new" but you can update this as the lead progresses.

Required fields include:
- Name: The contact's name
- Company: The company they represent
- Email: Their contact email
- Phone: Their contact phone number

Optional fields include:
- Job title: Their position at the company
- Website: Company website URL
- Address: Physical address information
- Notes: Additional information about the lead

## Lead Statuses

Lead statuses help you track where each lead is in your sales process:

- New: Recently added leads that haven't been contacted yet
- Contacted: Leads that have been reached out to
- Qualified: Leads that show genuine interest and fit your criteria
- Proposal: Leads that have received a quote or proposal
- Won: Converted leads that became customers
- Lost: Leads that didn't convert

## Lead Filtering and Search

Use the filtering and search capabilities to find specific leads quickly. You can filter by:
- Status: Show leads in specific stages
- Source: Filter by where the lead came from
- Date: Show leads created within a specific timeframe
- Tags: Filter by assigned tags

## Bulk Operations

Perform bulk operations on multiple leads at once:
- Assign to team members
- Update status for multiple leads
- Delete multiple leads
- Export lead data to CSV
