## Module Interconnections

The modules in Lynka CRM are designed to work together seamlessly:

<Steps>
<Step title="Lead creation**:
  All customer relationships begin with a lead record.
</Step>

<Step title="Opportunity conversion**:
  Qualified leads become opportunities in your sales pipeline.
</Step>

<Step title="Quote generation**:
  Opportunities lead to quotes when proposals are made.
</Step>

<Step title="Invoice creation**:
  Accepted quotes convert to invoices for billing.
</Step>

<Step title="Product integration**:
  Products are used across quotes and invoices.
</Step>

<Step title="Activity tracking**:
  All interactions are tracked across modules.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Data Flow Between Modules

<Tip>
  **Connected data**: Information entered in one module is available in related modules for consistency.
</Tip>

1. **Leads** become **Opportunities** when they show interest
2. **Opportunities** lead to **Quotes** when proposals are made
3. **Quotes** convert to **Invoices** when accepted
4. **Products** are used in both **Quotes** and **Invoices**
5. **Tasks** and **Activities** track progress across all modules

## Lead Management Module

### Features and Functionality

The lead management module is the foundation of your CRM system:

- **Lead capture**: Multiple methods for adding new leads
- **Lead scoring**: Automated lead qualification and scoring
- **Lead assignment**: Automatic assignment based on rules
- **Lead nurturing**: Automated follow-up sequences
- **Lead tracking**: Complete history of lead interactions
- **Lead conversion**: Easy conversion to opportunities

#### Lead Statuses
- **New**: Recently added leads that haven't been contacted
- **Contacted**: Leads that have been reached out to
- **Qualified**: Leads that show genuine interest and fit criteria
- **Proposal**: Leads that have received a quote or proposal
- **Won**: Converted leads that became customers
- **Lost**: Leads that didn't convert

### Opportunity Management Module

#### Sales Pipeline Tracking

The opportunity module helps you track potential deals:

- **Pipeline stages**: Customizable stages that match your sales process
- **Deal value tracking**: Track potential revenue for each opportunity
- **Probability management**: Set win probability for forecasting
- **Close date prediction**: Estimate when deals will close
- **Competitor tracking**: Monitor competitive threats
- **Stage history**: Track movement between pipeline stages

#### Opportunity Stages
- **New**: Initial opportunity created
- **Qualified**: Opportunity validated with clear requirements
- **Proposition**: Formal proposal submitted
- **Won**: Deal closed successfully
- **Lost**: Opportunity didn't materialize

## Quote Management Module

### Professional Proposal Creation

Create and manage professional quotes and proposals:

- **Template system**: Professional templates for consistent branding
- **Line item management**: Detailed product and service line items
- **Pricing tools**: Automatic calculation of totals and taxes
- **Discount management**: Apply discounts and special pricing
- **Approval workflows**: Approve quotes before sending to clients
- **Status tracking**: Track quote status (draft, sent, viewed, accepted, rejected)

#### Quote Statuses
- **Draft**: Quote being prepared
- **Sent**: Quote delivered to client
- **Viewed**: Client has opened the quote
- **Accepted**: Client has approved the quote
- **Rejected**: Client declined the quote
- **Expired**: Quote validity period ended
- **Void**: Quote cancelled
- **Paid**: Payment received

### Invoice Management Module

#### Billing and Payment Tracking

Handle the billing process for completed work:

- **Quote conversion**: Convert accepted quotes to invoices
- **Payment tracking**: Monitor payment status and due dates
- **Recurring billing**: Set up automated recurring invoices
- **Payment processing**: Accept payments directly through the system
- **Overdue management**: Track and manage overdue invoices
- **Reporting**: Detailed reporting on revenue and payments

#### Invoice Statuses
- **Draft**: Invoice being prepared
- **Posted**: Invoice issued to client
- **Paid**: Full payment received
- **Void**: Invoice cancelled
- **Partially Paid**: Partial payment received
- **Refunded**: Payment refunded to client

- **Pricing management**: Set and manage product pricing
- **Inventory tracking**: Monitor product availability
- **Category management**: Organize products into categories

#### Product Information

- **Brand**: Product brand information
