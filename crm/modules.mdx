## How Modules Connect

The modules in Lynka CRM are interconnected to provide a seamless experience:

1. **Leads** become **Opportunities** when they show interest
2. **Opportunities** lead to **Quotes** when proposals are made
3. **Quotes** convert to **Invoices** when accepted
4. **Products** are used in both **Quotes** and **Invoices**

This flow ensures that all customer interactions are tracked and connected throughout the entire sales process.

## Getting Started with Each Module

<Steps>
<Step title="Start with Leads">
  Begin by importing or adding your existing leads to the system. This is the foundation of your CRM.
</Step>

<Step title="Move to Opportunities">
  As leads show interest, convert them to opportunities to track them through your sales pipeline.
</Step>

<Step title="Create Quotes">
  When opportunities are ready for proposals, create quotes with your products and services.
</Step>

<Step title="Generate Invoices">
  Once quotes are accepted, convert them to invoices for billing.
</Step>

<Step title="Track Everything">
  Use tasks and activities to stay organized and maintain consistent follow-up.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Best Practices

<Tip>
  Start with one module and gradually incorporate others. Don't try to use everything at once.
</Tip>

<Info>
  All modules share customer data, so information entered in one module is available in others.
</Info>

<Warning>
  Regular data maintenance ensures accurate reporting across all modules.
</Warning>
