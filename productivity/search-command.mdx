Search exists at two levels in Lynka: quick navigation from the app shell and focused record search inside product areas.

## Global search and command access

The app header exposes the shared search or command control. Use it when you know the record or destination you want but do not want to navigate through several tabs first.

Search results can route to the relevant record detail, drawer or product area when the result contains the required routing context.

## Search inside lists

Lead, deal, company, invoice, product and other entity pages use the shared search, filter, sort and scope model.

Confirmed search terms can become search chips. Filters and sorting are applied together with the workspace, status, result-page, permission, and scope rules that apply to you.

On desktop, these controls sit alongside tables or Kanban views. On mobile, they are condensed into mobile search, filter and sort controls.

## Search Builder

Lynka also has a separate Search Builder surface for more deliberate query construction and saved or repeatable searches where the feature is exposed.

## While results refresh

Existing rows should remain visible during background fetching. A skeleton belongs to the initial state before usable results exist, not every time a filter refreshes.
